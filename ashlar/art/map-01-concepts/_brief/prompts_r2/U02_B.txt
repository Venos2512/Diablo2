SUBJECT (canon): Skeleton assembled from mismatched ossuary bones: a woman's skull on a man's frame, one arm longer than the other, rotted grave-goods as armour. Sword-and-shield melee; reassembles once unless the skull is broken; still mutters the Rite of Laying. Prologue monster: NO accent colour.
VARIANT B DESIGN: LOW, WIDE, braced stance. Broad heavy-boned frame with a bent-kneed wide stance and a head-forward hunch; a woman's skull wearing a half-helmet made from a rotted iron cooking pot. The RIGHT arm is the long one, held forward gripping a short heavy sword low; the left arm is short and carries a large tall kite-shaped shield made from one church-door plank that covers most of the torso in FRONT view. Rotted grave-goods armour: a wide belt-plate and one wooden shoulder board on the left only. Pose words in this design describe the 3/4 view only.
DIABLO DESIGN CUES: Diablo skeleton-warrior language: thick stylized bones, an oversized skull with deep dark eye sockets, mismatched rusted armour pieces strapped on, a ragged cloth tabard, an aggressive guard stance.
RAGGED (skeleton cloth): the rotted tabard, loincloth or hood rag is cut into several big jagged strips; armour pieces are dented and mismatched; still big clean painted shapes, no texture noise.
COLOUR (clear colour blocking): PRIMARY (about 60% of the figure): aged bone ivory #D8CCB0 (the skeleton). ACCENT: none (Prologue rule: no saturated accent colour; the pale neutral is the brightest touch).
