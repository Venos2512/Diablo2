SUBJECT (canon): Fen-drowned corpse bloated green-white, skin drum-tight, eels moving underneath. Lumbers toward the hero and bursts, spilling biting poisonous eels. Act I (fens, Rook's Weir, Willow Mere).
VARIANT B DESIGN: TOP-HEAVY bloated drowned fisher: huge swollen chest and upper arms ballooning a heavy oilskin coat, narrow hips and short thin legs, head sunk between the shoulders, a fishing net with three round stones wrapped diagonally over one shoulder as ONE broad net mass (no mesh detail, just a wide cord band). Three large eel-shaped bulges visible under the taut coat/skin on the back. DIFFERENTIATION: Top-heavy, shoulders widest, net-and-stone sash (contrast: variant A is belly-pear shaped with tails from the mouth). Variant A is designed independently with a different silhouette, shape structure, cloth/armour construction and accessory placement; do not reuse any of it, and keep the same canonical identity, size and signature prop or behaviour. Pose words in this design describe the 3/4 view only.
DIABLO DESIGN CUES: Diablo zombie and ghoul language: hunched heavy shoulders, sunken chest, very long arms with oversized grasping clawed hands, slack or gaping jaw, knobbly joints, protruding spine and ribs through torn holes, a dragging threatening gait; menacing, not pitiful.
RAGGED AND STYLIZED (undead zombie, required): the burial clothes are heavily tattered: cut into big jagged stylized rag strips and shredded hems (five to nine chunky dangling strips per garment), large torn holes showing grey flesh, ribs or spine, a ripped sleeve, a hanging collar flap. Stylized exaggeration: long arms, oversized hands, hunched spine, knobbly joints. Tatters are BIG chunky painted shapes with clean edges (Diablo Immortal style), never micro-fraying, thread noise or grunge texture. In the T-pose views keep the strips hanging close to the body so arms and legs stay readable.
COLOUR (clear colour blocking): PRIMARY (about 60% of the figure): drowned blue-green corpse skin #5E6B5A (the bloated body). SECONDARY (about 30%): dark peat-brown rags #3B3127. ACCENT (10% or less): poppy red #B3262A (coarse stitches across the drum-tight belly). Primary and secondary must read as two clearly different colours at thumbnail size (different hue and value). Everything else stays muted, damp Act I Diablo II mood.
WEAPON: none; the 3/4 view shows a menacing ready stance with empty hands. There is no weapon column.
POSE: 3/4 = front-left three-quarter presentation view in a menacing, readable ready stance. LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT and BACK show the SAME body in a strict T-pose: arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height, palms down, hands EMPTY; legs straight, feet slightly apart and flat on the floor; head level. In the LEFT and RIGHT views the arms are seen END-ON: the near arm points straight at the viewer and the far arm straight away, so both read as short foreshortened shapes at the shoulder; they never hang down and never reach forward or backward along the facing direction. Keep the creature's own spine posture (a hunched creature stays hunched) so its top stays on the dashed line; only the arms change. Extra or oversized arms also extend straight out sideways without overlapping anything.
