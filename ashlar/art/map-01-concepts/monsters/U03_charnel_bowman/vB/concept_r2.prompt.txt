SUBJECT (canon): Mismatched skeleton with a yew bow and a quiver of rusted grave-nails. Works in pairs and fights at range by retreating while shooting and keeping its distance (it does NOT fly a literal kite). Act I churchyard monster, no accent needed.
VARIANT B DESIGN: COMPACT and CROUCHED archer, weight low and leaning back as if stepping away while shooting. A skull of a different size from the shoulders (oversized head, narrow ribcage), a short recurve-style yew bow held in the right hand, and the arrows held in a belt-slung ossuary-jar quiver at the right hip with large rusted nail heads showing (four at most). A rotted grave-shroud scrap draped ONE side only over the left shoulder as a broad panel. Bent knees, long forearms. DIFFERENTIATION: Crouched, compact, hip-jar quiver and one-side shroud panel (contrast: variant A is tall and lean with a back quiver). Variant A is designed independently with a different silhouette, shape structure, cloth/armour construction and accessory placement; do not reuse any of it, and keep the same canonical identity, size and signature prop or behaviour. Pose words in this design describe the 3/4 view only.
DIABLO DESIGN CUES: Diablo skeleton-warrior language: thick stylized bones, an oversized skull with deep dark eye sockets, mismatched rusted armour pieces strapped on, a ragged cloth tabard, an aggressive guard stance.
RAGGED (skeleton cloth): the rotted tabard, loincloth or hood rag is cut into several big jagged strips; armour pieces are dented and mismatched; still big clean painted shapes, no texture noise.
COLOUR (clear colour blocking): PRIMARY (about 60% of the figure): aged bone ivory #D8CCB0 (the skeleton). SECONDARY (about 30%): dark yew-wood and leather brown #5A4A36 (bow, quiver, straps, hood rag). ACCENT (10% or less): poppy red #B3262A (fletching and one arm band). Primary and secondary must read as two clearly different colours at thumbnail size (different hue and value). Everything else stays muted, damp Act I Diablo II mood.

POSE: 3/4 = front-left three-quarter presentation view in a menacing, readable ready stance. LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT and BACK show the SAME body in a strict T-pose: arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height, palms down, hands EMPTY; legs straight, feet slightly apart and flat on the floor; head level. In the LEFT and RIGHT views the arms are seen END-ON: the near arm points straight at the viewer and the far arm straight away, so both read as short foreshortened shapes at the shoulder; they never hang down and never reach forward or backward along the facing direction. Keep the creature's own spine posture (a hunched creature stays hunched) so its top stays on the dashed line; only the arms change. Extra or oversized arms also extend straight out sideways without overlapping anything.
