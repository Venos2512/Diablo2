STYLE CORE (ASHLAR art direction, mandatory): moderately stylized hand-painted dark fantasy in the spirit of Diablo Immortal (the Diablo III / Diablo Immortal family look). NOT photoreal, NOT Diablo IV realism, NOT cartoon, chibi, anime, cel-shaded or toy-like. Proportions follow real anatomy; exaggeration goes only to the identity features (heroes: hands, feet and class props slightly oversized; monsters: their signature features pushed further). Strong readable silhouette, clear value grouping, clean colour blocking: a dominant colour, a secondary colour and a small accent, plus neutrals. Surfaces are SIMPLE: broad painted planes with soft gradients and a few crisp highlights; detail comes from big shapes (large torn edges, plates, straps, bones, cracks, patches), never from texture. No micro texture: no pores, fabric weave, leather grain, scratches, stains, speckle noise, fine grunge or photoreal PBR. Low detail never means flat: keep clear 3D form shading with a lit side, a shadow side, big soft fold shadows and darker occlusion in deep folds. No black cartoon outlines.

SHEET RULES: flat warm mid-grey studio backdrop, no environment, minimal soft contact shadows. Neutral studio light from above and slightly in front, the same in every view, with clear form shading (never a flat, evenly lit look); no bloom, no coloured light. No VFX and no particles unless stated. Every view shows the SAME design, proportions, colours and asymmetries; one-sided details stay on the same anatomical side in every view. LEFT = the subject's anatomical left side toward the camera, facing image-left; RIGHT = the true opposite side, facing image-right, never a mirror of LEFT; BACK = the true back, never a mirrored front. Model views use an eye-level orthographic camera. Nothing cropped, nothing overlapping. Concept art only: not a sprite, not a 3D model sheet with wireframes. Text: only the exact title and labels given; no other text, no watermark.

CONTENT: no crosses, no real saints, prayers or religious symbols; ASHLAR's church sign is the Wedge (a keystone shape), only where the brief asks for it. No gore emphasis and no blood splatter (Act III blood accents are small painted stains only); heroes show no wounds and no blood. Adults only; no nudity, no anatomical detail, no sexualised poses; no likeness of any real person. Never stage hanged bodies.

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE full-body single 3/4 character concept, entire figure and routine props in frame with generous margins. NO turnaround, NO additional views, NO text at all, NO scenery. Flat warm mid-grey background. Costume design is a PROPOSAL, not approved canon. PRIORITY: clean broad soft painterly planes, large fold shadows, smooth low-detail surfaces like Diablo Immortal promotional class art. Absolutely no cloth weave, distressed leather, tiny scratches, stippling, grunge, noise, photoreal texture, or high-frequency brush texture. Crisp silhouette but soft painted interior planes. Few large purposeful repairs only. Clear mature adult anatomy, not cartoon. Act I muted olive/peat/fog-grey palette, no sunlight and no pure white except Mercy linen.
Surface enforcement: REMOVE ALL wear, rust, patina, scratches and mottled brush marks. Metal uses uniform painted planes with broad lighting gradients. Leather and cloth use untextured matte colour fields and big soft fold shadows. Large repair patches are single smooth shapes. No weathering anywhere. Hair in broad locks.
Paint with the simplicity and smooth soft gradients of Diablo Immortal in-game character concept art; NOT detailed oil painting. Never crosshatch, never scumble. No tiny skin or textile lines. Calm untextured colour fields inside the large shapes.
NON-NEGOTIABLE rendering: perfectly smooth airbrush-like soft painted gradients inside each large form, no canvas grain, no weave, no brush texture. Broad clean gradients are crucial.
