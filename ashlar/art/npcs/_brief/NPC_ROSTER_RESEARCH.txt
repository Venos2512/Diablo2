=== 1. HARD RULES FROM CANON ===

- Five hubs with the same town roles (BIBLE 7.9, CAST 10): Lychgate (Act I),
  Lowcourse/Sepulcra (II), Namakhar (III), Vesnagrad (IV), Wrackhaven (V).
  Each hub has: Spiritual leader, Military leader, Smith, Trader, Guide, Keeper of lore.
  Names differ from Diablo; the roles are kept (BIBLE 3.1, "New culture, same town roles").
- Hubs are "vendors and quest-givers, no combat except scripted raids" (LD 1.4 / L83).
- Vendors are busy with something tied to the dead: "Routines, not kiosks" (LD L438).
  Jokes belong to the vendors (BIBLE 17.8; LD L978).
- From Act III on, every hub needs NPC REACTION STATES tied to Perfection trees (LD L579).
- Act V: NPC/town layers change per act (LD L590; BIOME doc). Plan the after-state now.
- Hub light is small and flickering (LD L439). A steady beautiful light = a Perfection.
- Art direction (ashlar/art-direction/artdirection.md L183) for humanoid NPC sheets: use the humanoid block.
  The image rules in ashlar/art-direction/artdirection.md (sec. 3 prompt blocks, sec. 11 QA) govern all images.
- Hero-concept rule that also binds NPCs (ashlar/docs/ASHLAR_HERO_CONCEPT_VI.md L859, L1013):
  Frostwife NPCs wear veils pulled forward over brow or face, and long fur cloaks to the
  floor, so they never look like the hero (Zorya: over seventy, white braids).
  This is marked as a PROPOSAL in that file (§12 item 16).
- Naming: ASHLAR names are original; Diablo names are never used (BIBLE L1639).

=== 5. DESIGN CONSTRAINTS PER NPC TYPE (for the concept brief) ===

- Every hub role must read at a glance (silhouette per role): smith = apron/forge tools;
  trader = barrow/stock/coats; guide = travel gear; lore = books/ink/charts; spiritual
  = vestments/veil; military = armour/lamellar/brigandine.
- Keep the Diablo archetype idea of each role, change name, origin, material, palette
  (BIBLE 3.1; CAST 12 table approach). Do not copy a Diablo design.
- Light: NPCs lit by small flickering hub light, not steady white (LD L439).
- Act III+ reaction states needed in each hub (LD L579): plan a "before Perfection" and
  "after Perfection" look for the same NPC.
- Act-state looks: hubs change after each act (LD L590; ASHLAR_BIOME doc "after" state).
  NPC concepts must be drawn with "routine" props (LD L438: Gennet writes names, Brannoc
  feeds Edda broth, Ansel casts bells, Pell rings the alarm).
- Women: tasteful, not sexualised, for Nareh, Ignatia, Tamsin, Zorya, Dragana, Maelys, Rozenn,
  Mila (age). Follow ASHLAR_HERO_CONCEPT v4 rule "tasteful allure" only if the parent confirms.
- Veils (Frostwives, Zorya): veil pulled forward, long fur cloaks to floor (HERO_CONCEPT L859).
- Children (Mila, Bozhena, counting children, Fen Reed): art-direction review; keep dignified.
