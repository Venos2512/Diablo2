Use case: game-ui-kit sheet. Image 1 is the approved PC HUD layer of ASHLAR ("Until the Last"), an original dark-fantasy isometric action RPG, on black: style and material reference only. Match its red life globe at the left (glass sphere with red liquid in a round carved stone ring), its matte grey dressed stone, pale mortar and dark iron, and its level of detail; do not copy its layout.

SHEET: 16:9 landscape, flat dark warm grey backdrop (#2B2825), no environment, soft top-left light, clear form shading. Title at the top: "FL FLAGELLANT — LIFE GLOBE AND WOUND". Main area: six equal cells in two rows of three, each a life globe in its round carved stone ring (no statue), all the same size and angle, with a label under each. Bottom strip: three smaller globes in a row.

THE STORY: the Flagellant pays skills with her own life. The spent life becomes WOUND: it can still be won back for 10 seconds, then it is lost. The globe therefore has an extra layer above the red liquid: "life that can still be won back", fading. The cost is shown on the interface only, never on the hero.

CELL 1, label "FULL": red liquid at 90 percent, glass intact.
CELL 2, label "WOUND": solid red liquid up to 55 percent. Directly above it, from 55 to 80 percent, a band of darker, semi-transparent, faded red, clearly separated from the solid liquid by a thin pale line. Over that band only, the glass carries three or four large, clean painted cracks (smooth lines, never splintered); the glass above and below the band is intact.
CELL 3, label "HEALING": same, but the faded band is short (55 to 64 percent) and only two short cracks remain, fading out.
CELL 4, label "FLOOR 10%": red liquid at 10 percent. A small dark iron staple is fixed on the stone ring on both sides at the 10 percent height, with a thin carved line across the glass at that height. No red alarm glow, no flashing border.
CELL 5, label "SHORED": red liquid at 65 percent; directly above it, from 65 to 85 percent, an opaque band of pale grey mortar with two or three fitted-stone joint lines, like a course of masonry laid on top of the liquid (a shield), and three small stone wedges set into the outer ring.
CELL 6, label "REDUCED IMAGERY": like cell 2, but the band above the liquid is a flat, matte ash-grey layer with NO cracks at all.
BOTTOM STRIP, three small globes with labels "PROLOGUE", "ACT I", "ACT III": liquid of grey mortar slurry (matte, not red); red liquid; red liquid with a few fine white-gold threads drifting in it. All at 80 percent.

Text: only the title and the labels given. Simple, large shapes; no micro texture.
AVOID: blood splatter, drips, gore, wounds or bodies, skulls, crosses, haloes, gold filigree, gemstones, glossy chrome, neon, bloom, spiky or scratchy cracks.
