HUD LAYOUT (variant PC-B2 "LOW SEGMENTAL ARCH"): bottom centre, about 44% of the screen width and at most 14% of its height. One very SHALLOW segmental stone arch, like a low old bridge, never like a doorway: the arch ring rises only about 40 px from its ends to the top of the keystone. Its LEFT SPRINGER (the base stone on the left) holds the LIFE GLOBE; its RIGHT SPRINGER holds the EXHUMER VESSEL; the globe and the vessel are the highest points of the cluster, and the top of the keystone is level with them or slightly lower. The arch ring between them is one row of wedge-shaped voussoir sockets: LMB, RMB and 1 rising gently on the left, the Q keystone at the apex, then 2, 3 and 4 descending gently on the right. Under the arch, in its low opening, the four small universal sockets SPACE, F, R, T sit in one short row resting on the bottom edge of the screen.
