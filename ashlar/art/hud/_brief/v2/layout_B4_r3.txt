HUD LAYOUT (variant PC-B4 "SUNKEN ARCH"): bottom centre, about 39% of the screen width and at most 11% of its height. The top of a wide, very flat stone arch that has sunk into the ground, like an old bridge buried to its crown: only the upper band of the arch ring shows, and both of its ends curve gently down and disappear below the bottom edge of the screen. Along this visible band runs one row of voussoir sockets, from left to right: SPACE and F (small, low on the left end, where the ring meets the bottom edge), LMB, RMB, 1, the Q keystone at the crown (the highest stone, its top at most about 110 px above the bottom edge), 2, 3, 4, then R and T (small, low on the right end). Outside the two ends of the arch, as two separate round stone settings resting on the bottom edge: the LIFE GLOBE on the left and the EXHUMER VESSEL on the right, both whole (never cut by the screen edge), their tops level with the keystone's top. Under the crown there is no opening: the screen edge cuts the arch.
