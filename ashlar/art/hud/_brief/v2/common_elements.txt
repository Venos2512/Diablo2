HUD ELEMENTS (same in every variant of this family):
- LIFE GLOBE: diameter about 12% of the screen height (about 130 px). Act I state: deep heart-red liquid with a soft living sheen, filled to about 60%, with a thin grey mortar sediment at the very bottom. Small text "248 / 395" on a tiny dark plate under or beside it, inside the bottom 15%.
- EXHUMER VESSEL: THE LANTERN, about 110 px tall: a small hooded iron lantern drawn front-on, with a glass oil reservoir at its base showing the oil level at about 70%; down its side five notches for the five wick settings, the third notch lit; inside, a small flickering warm flame.
- SEVEN SKILL SOCKETS, each about 64-72 px: LMB a crossbow bolt; RMB a lantern beam; 1 a sprung trap; Q = the KEYSTONE, the DOUBT socket, a tapered wedge stone slightly larger than the others, in Act I sealed flush with grey mortar with a faint wedge outline and no icon; 2 a spade striking down; 3 a hook on a chain, ON COOLDOWN: dark clockwise sweep with the number "6"; 4 a coil of wire with a small bell. Mortar joints run between the stones.
- FOUR SMALL UNIVERSAL SOCKETS, each about 48 px: SPACE (Dodge: two charge pips, one full and one refilling), F (Draught: four small corked clay vials, all full), R (Osric's Lantern), T (Open a Face: an open palm pressed to stone).
- EXPERIENCE: a thin mortar joint running along the outer edge of the skill stones, filled about 35%; the level "9" in a small keystone tab at the joint's left end.
- CAPTION (accessibility): just above the centre of the cluster, one line in small italic serif on a dark translucent strip: "[a lullaby, far ahead]".
- TOP-LEFT: quest tracker on a dark translucent plate: title "THE COUNT OF THE SLEEPERS" and one line "Follow the lullaby".
- TOP-CENTRE: enemy nameplate "HUSH-WOLF" in chiseled capitals, with a thin dark-red health bar, full.
- TOP-RIGHT: the round minimap (about 13% of the screen height), no quest marker, only causeway lines in grey ink and the hero's dot; beneath it the zone plate "THE NIGHT FENS".
- CURSOR: a small dark iron pointer shaped like a coffin-nail, over the nearest Hush-Wolf.
- No full-width bar: the left and right quarters of the bottom edge stay empty and show the world.
