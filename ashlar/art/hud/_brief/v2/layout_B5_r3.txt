HUD LAYOUT (variant PC-B5 "INVERTED ARCH"): bottom centre, about 37% of the screen width and at most 11% of its height. An INVERTED ARCH (an upside-down arch, as builders lay in old foundations): a shallow stone curve that is LOWEST in the middle and rises gently toward its two ends, like a shallow smile or a hanging chain of wedge stones. The Q keystone sits at the very bottom centre, pointing down, its lower tip close to the bottom edge of the screen. The voussoir sockets climb gently outward from it: 1, RMB, LMB up to the left; 2, 3, 4 up to the right; the rise from the keystone to the ends is only about 40 px. The two ends of the curve are its highest points and rest on two round stone settings: the LIFE GLOBE on the left end and the EXHUMER VESSEL on the right end. The four small universal sockets sit outside the curve on the bottom edge: SPACE and F to the left of the life globe, R and T to the right of the vessel. The middle of the cluster is its lowest part, so the view above the centre is completely open.
