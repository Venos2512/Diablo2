HUD LAYOUT (variant PC-B3 "FLAT ARCH"): bottom centre, about 33% of the screen width and at most 11% of its height. A FLAT ARCH (a jack arch, as over an old window): a straight horizontal lintel made of wedge-shaped voussoir sockets whose side joints fan outward, all sitting in one straight row: LMB, RMB, 1, the Q keystone in the centre, 2, 3, 4. The keystone is only slightly taller than its neighbours (it drops about 9 px lower than the row and rises no more than 6 px above it). The lintel rests on two square stone piers at its ends: the LEFT PIER holds the LIFE GLOBE, the RIGHT PIER holds the EXHUMER VESSEL; their tops are level with the top of the lintel. Below the lintel, in the low opening between the piers, the four small universal sockets SPACE, F, R, T sit in one short row resting on the bottom edge of the screen. The whole cluster reads as one low, wide, flat stone frame.
