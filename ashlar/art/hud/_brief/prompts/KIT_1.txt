SHARED ASHLAR HUD DESIGN LANGUAGE (applies to every HUD element in this image; canon: "interface frames of dressed stone and grey mortar" and "a serif typeface with chiseled letterforms, as if cut into stone"):
- MATERIAL: frames, plates, rings and sockets are cut from matte dressed grey stone (squared ashlar blocks with faint chisel tool-marks) joined by recessed pale-grey mortar joints. The courses are hand-laid and very slightly uneven, never machine-perfect, never polished. Quiet, dark and low in contrast: frames never glow and never compete with the game.
- HUMAN REPAIR is the signature motif ("seams are life"): here and there a small mended crack in the stone held by a dark iron staple, or a patch of newer mortar; a few round iron coffin-nail heads used as rivets. Restrained: at most one repair per element.
- ALLOWED MOTIFS: the keystone wedge (a tapered arch stone), arch voussoirs, coffin-nails, mortar joints, a faint human handprint pressed into mortar. FORBIDDEN: crosses or any real-world religious symbol, pentagrams, demon horns, skull-encrusted or bone-spiked frames, gold filigree, gemstones, glossy bevels, chrome or brushed metal, neon, sci-fi panels, fantasy-MMO gold trims.
- COLOUR: charcoal and slate backing (desaturated), stone greys and pale mortar, bone and parchment text. Colour appears only where it carries information: the life liquid, the vocation vessel, loot rarity text, the cooldown sweep and the enemy health bar. Most of the HUD must read in grayscale. No large gold or red areas.
- TYPE: titles and zone names in an inscriptional serif with chiseled, wedge-cut letterforms, like letters cut into stone; numbers, keybinds and quest lines in a clean, highly legible book serif. Small, crisp and high in contrast, on dark plates. Keybind letters sit in tiny dark stone tabs at a socket's lower corner.
- ICONS: painted emblem icons in bone, ochre and iron tones on dark slate, one clear silhouette each, readable at small size; no tiny detail, no neon glow, nothing photo-real.
- LIFE GLOBE construction (canon: the globe shows what the hero's blood is, and its colour changes with the story): a clear glass sphere set in a ring of dressed stone voussoirs with mortar joints and a keystone at its top; the liquid level is the amount of life. Never a skull, a demon face or a gem.
- VOCATION VESSEL (canon): beside the life globe, each vocation has its own resource vessel that is read by SHAPE, not by colour, so it works in grayscale and for colour-blind players. It sits in a matching stone setting and is smaller than the globe.
- SKILLS AND KEYS (canon): six skill slots LMB, RMB, 1, 2, 3, 4, plus a sustained slot 5 for the Pallbearer and the Pyre-Singer only. Universal actions: DODGE (SPACE) with two charge pips; DOUBT (Q), a universal skill taught in Act II, which before that is a socket sealed flush with grey mortar, with a faint wedge outline and no icon; DRAUGHT (F), four healing charges shown as four small corked clay vials; LANTERN KEY (R), an icon of Osric's Lantern, a plain iron watch-lantern with one hinged hood flap; OPEN A FACE (T, the town portal), an icon of an open palm pressed to stone. Cooldowns are a dark clockwise sweep with a small number.
- EXPERIENCE: a thin mortar joint that fills with fresh pale mortar from left to right; the level number sits in a small keystone.
- MAP: the minimap is round, grey-ink lines on dark vellum like a surveyor's drawing, inside a ring of dressed stone; north is marked by a small wedge; the hero is a small pale dot; quest markers appear only where this prompt says so.
- RENDERING: hand-painted UI art at final production quality, crisp edges, readable at 1080p, consistent light from the upper left. The game world behind it stays painterly, broad and low in detail, like reference 1. The HUD must look like one designed system: every element shares the same stone, mortar, type and icon style.
