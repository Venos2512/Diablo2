ORNAMENT PAIR B "THE BEARER AND THE PERFECTION" (humans hold each other up; a Perfection is too perfect):
- LEFT, guarding the life globe: THE BEARER, a carved grey-stone human labourer kneeling on one knee at the outer left of the globe, a mason in a plain work smock and hood, carrying the globe's stone ring on his shoulders and raised hands like a heavy burden, head bowed under the weight; hand-cut, slightly uneven stone, with one mended crack held by a dark iron staple and a patch of newer pale mortar (human repair). Matte grey, never glowing.
- RIGHT, guarding the lantern globe: A PERFECTION, a tall, slender figure standing at the outer right of the globe, wrapped in smooth pale linen that falls in perfectly even folds; its body and face are seamless, smooth, pale salt-glass, perfectly symmetric and too clean, with no cracks and no joints at all; a featureless serene face; it holds the lantern globe's ring with two long still hands, as if offering it. It gives off a faint, steady, even pale light along its edges (never flickering). It faces left, toward the bearer. The contrast reads at a glance: rough, mended, labouring human stone on the left; seamless, flawless, beautiful but cold on the right.
