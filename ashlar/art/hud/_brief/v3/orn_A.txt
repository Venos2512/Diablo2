- RIGHT, guarding the lantern globe: a DISCARDED, a creature of fired clay: a hunched, heavy figure of dry matte brick-orange terracotta (like fired brick, lighter dusty ochre on the lit edges; never glossy, never chocolate-brown, never a robot or a golem of plates), one continuous clay body with two big curved clay horns and a long jaw, crouched at the outer right of the globe, one huge clay hand gripping the globe's stone ring from outside; three or four large cracks across its body with a faint red-orange kiln glow deep inside them, like coals. It faces left, toward the saint.
