ORNAMENT PAIR C "WEDGE AND BELL" (architecture instead of figures):
- LEFT, guarding the life globe: the globe's stone frame grows into a tall stepped stone buttress on its outer left side, crowned above the globe by one large KEYSTONE WEDGE (a tapered arch stone, ASHLAR's church sign) set on two short leaning voussoir stones that meet over the globe like a small arch; a faint human handprint pressed into the mortar of the buttress; three round iron coffin-nail heads as rivets; one mended crack held by a dark iron staple. Matte grey stone.
- RIGHT, guarding the lantern globe: a matching stepped stone buttress on the outer right side carrying a dark wrought-iron sexton's bracket that curls over the top of the globe; from it hangs a small iron soul-bell on a short chain, and a long sexton's grave-spade leans upright against the buttress (never crossed with anything). Matte stone and dark iron, no glow except the existing lantern flame inside the globe.
