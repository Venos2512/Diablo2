CLASS-SELECT HAND SHOWCASE KEY ART: one 16:9 wide painting, no text, no UI.
CONTEXT (canon): the class-choice screen of ASHLAR. Six hands reach out of a wall of skulls set in grey mortar, in near darkness, lit by one sexton's candle; the player chooses which hand to hold. This image is the moment right after the player has CHOSEN this hand: it has reached out of the wall toward the viewer and shows itself off, then offers itself to be held. Image 1 is the approved overview key art of the screen: match its skull wall (big simple skulls and long bones in grey mortar), its candle, its warm near-dark mood, palette and hand-painted rendering exactly.
CAMERA AND LAYOUT: a close three-quarter view from slightly ABOVE, looking down into the open palm. The chosen forearm comes out of the skull wall from the upper part of the frame, its sleeve emerging from between the skulls with the mortar closing around it, and the forearm reaches forward toward the viewer. The hand is the hero of the image: large, about 40-50 percent of the image height, the whole hand, wrist and the first part of the sleeve fully inside the frame with a clear margin; nothing cropped. Behind it the wall stays readable but dim, and one or two of the other five hands are visible only as dim shapes sinking into the dark at the sides. The candle (small, sooty, short, in a plain iron dish holder with a ring handle on a rough stone ledge) sits low in the frame, slightly off-centre, and is the main warm light; the class effect described below is the second, smaller light. Composition: one warm light point and one big dark mass; the darkest values are deep warm black, but the skulls and every mark of the hand stay readable.
POSE (most important, user decision; the previous drafts failed it because the hand hung down with the palm facing the viewer): the chosen hand is turned PALM UP. The forearm comes forward out of the wall and the hand is fully supinated: the open palm faces UP toward the ceiling and is clearly seen from slightly above and in front, like a hand held out to be taken, or held out to receive something. Fingers relaxed, slightly spread and gently curled like a shallow cup, the forearm reaches forward almost horizontally, the wrist stays level, and the fingers point toward the viewer and curl slightly UP at the tips, so we look down into the cupped palm the way you look at a hand offered to you across a table. The back of the hand is NOT shown; its marks only show at the wrist and the outer edge of the hand. Calm, dignified, inviting; never a fist, never grabbing, never fingers pointing down. One left hand only: an adult hand of real anatomy, slightly oversized like ARPG hero hands, five fingers (four fingers and a thumb), clear gaps between the fingers.
EFFECTS: the class effect is small, hand-painted and close to the hand (a few big readable shapes, not a cloud of tiny particles); no beams, no magic circles, no runes, no halo, no bloom, no white-gold glow.
THE HAND: the Pallbearer's left hand, a broad gauntlet of polished silver plate exactly like the Pallbearer in image 3 (bright steel plates, brass rivets and trims), with a row of FOUR big round silver coffin-nail heads on the knuckle plate; at the wrist a short purple pall-cloth cuff with a white border (the purple and white of his tabard in image 3). Line: "Lift with the legs. And with the heart."
SHOWCASE MOMENT: the heavy gauntlet lies open PALM UP: the broad worn leather palm patch with ONE dark rope groove across it faces up, the steel finger plates curve up around it like a cup; the row of four silver coffin-nail heads is seen in profile along the top edge of the knuckle plate, catching a crisp candlelit highlight; a little pale stone grit and grey mortar dust falls from the knuckle plates in a few chunky flakes, as if the hand has just pulled free of the Wall. The metal never glows: only reflected warm candlelight and a soft cool rim on the plates. Strong, protective, steady.
COLOURS: steel #C8CBCB and #6E7276, purple #5B2A86 with a white border, brass #B08A3E; warm candle #E0A050.
