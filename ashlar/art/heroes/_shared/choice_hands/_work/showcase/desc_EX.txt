CLASS-SELECT HAND SHOWCASE KEY ART: one 16:9 wide painting, no text, no UI.
CONTEXT (canon): the class-choice screen of ASHLAR. Six hands reach out of a wall of skulls set in grey mortar, in near darkness, lit by one sexton's candle; the player chooses which hand to hold. This image is the moment right after the player has CHOSEN this hand: it has reached out of the wall toward the viewer and shows itself off, then offers itself to be held. Image 1 is the approved overview key art of the screen: match its skull wall (big simple skulls and long bones in grey mortar), its candle, its warm near-dark mood, palette and hand-painted rendering exactly.
CAMERA AND LAYOUT: a close, slightly low three-quarter view. The chosen forearm comes out of the skull wall from the upper part of the frame, its sleeve emerging from between the skulls with the mortar closing around it, and the forearm reaches forward toward the viewer. The hand is the hero of the image: large, about 40-50 percent of the image height, the whole hand, wrist and the first part of the sleeve fully inside the frame with a clear margin; nothing cropped. Behind it the wall stays readable but dim, and one or two of the other five hands are visible only as dim shapes sinking into the dark at the sides. The candle (small, sooty, short, in a plain iron dish holder with a ring handle on a rough stone ledge) sits low in the frame, slightly off-centre, and is the main warm light; the class effect described below is the second, smaller light. Composition: one warm light point and one big dark mass; the darkest values are deep warm black, but the skulls and every mark of the hand stay readable.
POSE: the motion ends in an inviting OFFER: the hand open, calm and dignified, fingers relaxed and slightly spread, turning toward the viewer as if to be taken and held; never a fist, never grabbing, never clawing or threatening. One left hand only: an adult hand of real anatomy, slightly oversized like ARPG hero hands, five fingers, clear gaps between the fingers.
EFFECTS: the class effect is small, hand-painted and close to the hand (a few big readable shapes, not a cloud of tiny particles); no beams, no magic circles, no runes, no halo, no bloom, no white-gold glow.
THE HAND: the Exhumer's left hand in a dark brown oil-soaked leather glove exactly like the gloves and bracers of the Exhumer in image 3 (a flared leather gauntlet cuff with brass corner plates), the dark green coat sleeve above it; a pale worn patch on the thumb where the crossbow string rests. Line: "Keep your light low. The things worth killing see better than you."
SHOWCASE MOMENT: with a flick of the thumb a SMALL warm lamp flame (no bigger than the candle flame) catches on the oiled glove just above the cupped palm, lighting the glossy oil sheen of the leather from below; a thin wisp of grey smoke rises from it; the brass trims catch the light. Watchful, practical, light kept low.
COLOURS: oiled leather #5B4130 and #3A2A1E, coat green #2E4A3A, brass #B08A3E, lamp flame #F2A24A; warm candle #E0A050.
