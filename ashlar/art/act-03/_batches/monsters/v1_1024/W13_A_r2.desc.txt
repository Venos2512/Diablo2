SUBJECT (canon): eel-men left behind when the sea died: slick scales, fins, lidless eyes, hiding in the bilges of the wrecks. They constrict and bite. Coil-Seers among them carry coral staffs and cast brine magic. Design rule: NOT a human torso on a snake body (avoid the naga look): a Coilborn stands on two short bowed legs, its long eel tail trails behind from the base of the spine.
BUILD (Act I recipe): THREE big masses: (1) a hunched wiry eel-man body with slick teal scales #3F8C88, a paler grey-teal belly, long arms with oversized webbed clawed hands, short bowed legs and the long thick eel tail; (2) one tall dark #2F5A52 dorsal fin running from the skull down the back into the tail, plus fin flaps at the forearms; (3) a long eel head with a wide jaw of needle teeth and round lidless pale eyes. A rope belt with one rusted ship's hook #8A4B2D. At most three to five large marks per figure (big cracks, big chips, big salt crusts); never many small tears, holes, notches or speckles.
ACT III COLOUR: salt and bone white is MATTE, dusty and dirty-edged, never glowing, no bloom, no sparkle. The only red allowed anywhere is blood #9E1B1B as one or two small painted stains or drips, never on materials, eyes or anything glowing. No other red, no orange, no green, no coloured light. White-on-white readability: the creature keeps dark, rust #8A4B2D or teal #3F8C88 areas and strong value contrast so it reads on white salt ground. Primary teal scales, secondary dark sea-teal fins #2F5A52 and rust belt; Coil-Seer adds a salt-stiff grey sailcloth hood and a branching coral staff #B59A5B. Accent one small blood stain #9E1B1B on the teeth.
SILHOUETTE A: hunched, long-snouted, one tall fin ridge and a long trailing tail.
