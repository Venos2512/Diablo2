SUBJECT (canon): D03 KILN-BULL, a Discarded of the Flint Delvings: a giant horned brute of living fired clay with glowing nostrils, made like a walking battering ram that also batters gates in the siege of Act IV. It charges out of the dark; its hoofbeats are heard before it is seen. Diablo family: the D1 Horned Demon.

ANTI-ROBOT (most important): it is a LIVING ANIMAL BRUTE, never a golem robot, mech or suit of armour. The whole body is ONE continuous sculpted mass of fired clay, like a terracotta statue of a beast that came alive: real muscle anatomy modelled in the clay (bull neck hump, huge deltoids, chest, belly, thick forearm muscles, knuckles, calves), every form flows smoothly into the next. NO separate plates, NO panel lines, NO armour segments, NO dark gaps or sockets at the joints, NO mechanical or ball joints, NO rivets, NO geometric facets. Skin seams do not exist; only a few large natural kiln cracks.

BUILD (three big masses): (1) a huge hunched bull torso and neck hump that carries the head low and forward; (2) two enormous arms with massive forearms and heavy hands (four thick fingers and a thumb each, clay knuckles, no claws), hands hanging near knee height but off the floor; (3) two short, thick, digitigrade bull legs on large cloven hooves. Bull head with a broad muzzle, small deep-set eyes, two nostrils glowing kiln orange, thick horns. A short tufted clay tail. Proportions of a heavy Diablo brute: arms and shoulders pushed big, legs short and powerful.

SURFACE AND MATERIAL (most important after anti-robot; it must NOT look like chocolate): DRY, MATTE, DUSTY FIRED CLAY like a kiln-fired brick or a terracotta flowerpot, never glossy, never smooth melted, never syrupy. Broad painted planes with soft gradients; the lit planes turn pale and dusty (buff ochre #D8A47A on the highest highlights), the shadow planes stay warm red-brown; a faint dry powdery bloom on the top surfaces. Clay cues: two or three big CHIPPED edges (at a horn tip, a knuckle, an elbow) that show the paler orange inner clay; three to five large dry cracks on the whole body, with a faint kiln-orange glow deep inside only those cracks; soot is DRY SCORCH blackening in a few soft smudges (as if licked by kiln flames: around the nostrils, the shoulders, the lower legs), NEVER liquid, never drips, never a poured coating. No glaze drips anywhere. No small cracks, no crackle, no pits, no speckle, no spiky or scratchy strokes.

SCALE: height to the top of the horns exactly 1.40H in the neutral views (the dashed "top of horns 1.40H" line of the scale guide); the H silhouette is 262 px tall, so the standing views are about 367 px tall. Same scale in every view; the 3/4 crouch is lower.

POSES: 3/4 = an acted charging stance: head lowered, horns forward, one hoof pawing back, arms swung wide, ready to ram. LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK = neutral standing T-pose: upright, both arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height, hands open, legs straight under the hips, hooves flat. No weapon, no prop, no WEAPON cell.

SHEET: one row on the flat warm-grey backdrop like the scale guide: H, 3/4, LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK, labels exactly "H", "3/4", "LEFT", "RIGHT", "FRONT", "BACK"; keep the two dashed lines with their labels.
NEUTRAL VIEWS HEIGHT: in LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT and BACK it stands UPRIGHT (back straight, head raised and level, legs straight) so the horn tops touch the "top of horns 1.40H" line; H stays exactly 262 px. The groin and hips are covered by a heavy rough apron of dry soot-blackened clay, part of the body; muscles are 4-6 big smooth clay masses per limb, no fibre lines.
Title exactly: "D03 KILN-BULL — B".
VARIANT B SILHOUETTE: a broader, more upright brute built like a gate ram: a massive flat battering-ram forehead boss of thick scorched dry clay between short, thick horns that curve out and up like a bull's; a barrel chest and a wide belly; a small cold kiln mouth (an arched firebox opening with a faint orange glow) set into the middle of its upper back, part of the body, not a machine. Arms shorter but even thicker than A. Reads at thumbnail as a squat square block with a flat forehead and two up-curved horns.
