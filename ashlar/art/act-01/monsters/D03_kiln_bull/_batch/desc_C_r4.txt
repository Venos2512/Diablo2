SUBJECT (canon): D03 KILN-BULL, a Discarded of the Flint Delvings: a giant horned brute of living fired clay with glowing nostrils, made like a walking battering ram that also batters gates in the siege of Act IV. It charges out of the dark; its hoofbeats are heard before it is seen. Diablo family: the D1 Horned Demon.

DESIGN: EXACTLY the creature of image 4 (variant C, already chosen by the art lead): same head, wide flat buffalo horns, horn boss, heavy scorched clay mane, dry matte brick-orange clay body, cracks with faint kiln-orange glow, glowing nostrils, soot-blackened clay apron over the groin and hips, short tufted tail, short thick digitigrade legs on big cloven hooves, same colours and same low detail. Only the four model views (LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK) and the arm length change, as the POSES and FIXES say.

ANTI-ROBOT: a LIVING ANIMAL BRUTE, never a golem robot, mech or suit of armour. One continuous sculpted mass of fired clay with real muscle anatomy; NO plates, panel lines, armour segments, dark joint gaps, mechanical joints, rivets or facets.

SURFACE AND MATERIAL (it must NOT look like chocolate): DRY, MATTE, DUSTY FIRED CLAY like a kiln-fired brick, never glossy, never melted, never syrupy, as in image 4. Lit planes pale and dusty (buff ochre #D8A47A on the highest highlights), shadow planes warm red-brown; two or three big chipped edges showing paler inner clay; three to five large dry cracks with a faint kiln-orange glow inside; soot only as DRY scorch smudges, never drips. Muscles are 4-6 big smooth clay masses per limb, no fibre lines, no small cracks, no spiky or scratchy strokes.

ARMS (most important): a gorilla build with VERY LONG, enormous arms, the same arms as in the 3/4 view of image 4 where the knuckles reach the floor. Each arm measures about 0.75H from the shoulder joint to the knuckles (about 170 px; the H silhouette is 228 px tall): upper arm and forearm about equal, the forearm much thicker than the upper arm, a heavy hand at the end (four thick fingers and a thumb, clay knuckles, no claws). If the arms hung down, the fingertips would reach below the knees.

SCALE: height to the top of the horns exactly 1.40H in LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT and BACK: the horn tops touch the dashed "top of horns 1.40H" line of the scale guide (image 3); the H silhouette stays exactly 228 px, so the standing views are about 319 px tall. Same scale in every view; the 3/4 charge is lower.

POSES:
- 3/4 = keep the acted charging stance of image 4: head lowered, horns forward, knuckles of the long arms on the floor, one hoof pawing back.
- FRONT and BACK = a clean symmetric T-pose: torso UPRIGHT, back straight, head raised and level looking straight ahead, legs straight under the hips, hooves flat about shoulder width apart. Both arms leave the body at shoulder height and stretch straight out to the sides as two perfectly horizontal lines, no sagging, no bent elbows; hands open and flat, palms down, fingers together. Fingertip-to-fingertip span about 2.0H (about 460 px), clearly wider than the creature is tall. BACK is the true back of FRONT: the mane covers the upper back, the tail hangs at the centre.
- LEFT and RIGHT = the same T-pose seen exactly from the side (true profile of the head, horns, torso, legs and hooves, no turn toward the camera). The near arm points STRAIGHT AT THE CAMERA at shoulder height, so it is seen END-ON and strongly foreshortened: a big round shoulder mass with the open hand seen palm-down edge-on in front of it, overlapping the chest at shoulder height; the arm does NOT reach forward toward the muzzle, does not reach sideways and does not hang down. The far arm is hidden behind the body. Each side view is only about 0.95H wide.

SHEET: one row on the flat warm-grey backdrop like the scale guide: H, 3/4, LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK, labels exactly "H", "3/4", "LEFT", "RIGHT", "FRONT", "BACK", each view centred over its label; keep the two dashed lines with their labels. Wide FRONT and BACK columns hold the long arm span; nothing touches its neighbour.
Title exactly: "D03 KILN-BULL — C".
