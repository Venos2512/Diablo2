## Size (measured on the sheet against the H silhouette)
- H silhouette 439 px. All four model views 534-537 px = about 1.22H (frond crown top).
- Canon text: crown 1.75H, torso width 0.90H (the sheet is about 0.7x that). Game scale is set later in the lab.

## Silhouette and build
- Tall narrow columnar hulk of thick twisted dark root bundles; two root-column legs splaying into thick root toes on the ground.
- Signature asymmetry: a huge root-mass RIGHT arm (hanging past the knee, many root fingers) and a short LEFT stub arm with a small
  bone hand.
- Head: a small elongated ivory bone skull face at the front of the head under a drooping crown of olive willow fronds.
- A thick pale rope noose around the neck, knot under the skull, loop down the chest; a hollow ribcage cavity with big ivory rib arcs
  in the upper chest; a few flat moss patches.

## Materials and colours
Dark wet willow-root brown #4A3526 (primary), olive willow fronds and moss #6E7B45 (secondary), bone-ivory skull, ribs and pale rope
noose #D8CCB0 (accent).

## Asymmetry
RIGHT = huge root arm, LEFT = stub arm with bone hand (all five concept views agree). Moss on the upper right arm and on the left hip.

## What Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Many single willow leaves: three wide thick leaf-bundle clumps; at the front and sides they end above the shoulder line so the
  T-pose arms leave the body clear of the leaves; on the back they fall to about 40 %.
- Dangling thin roots and tendrils (arm root fingers, hanging root strands): thick bundled roots; the big arm ends in five thick root
  fingers along the arm line.
- Noose rope strands: one thick rope.
