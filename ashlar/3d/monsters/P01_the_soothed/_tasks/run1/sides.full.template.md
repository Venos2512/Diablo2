{{TPOSE_RULES}}

{{CONTENT}}

Use case: character turnaround for image-to-3D (Tripo multi-view input).
REFERENCE IMAGES (attached in this order): (1) the concept image of the creature: identity reference for the design and for what the side profile looks like (head, hat/horns, parts worn on the back, legs). (2) tpose_front_back.png: the APPROVED front/back turnaround of the same creature in the target STRICT T-POSE: identity reference for the pose, body proportions, materials and colours. Keep exactly the same design and the same pose. Do not redesign. Do not copy the lineup layout, the H silhouette or any text.
{{DESIGN}}
Canvas: one landscape 3:2 image (1536x1024 or larger) with TWO full-body views of the same creature side by side: left = the creature's LEFT side toward the camera, facing screen-left (true orthographic side profile, exactly 90 degrees from the front view); right = the creature's RIGHT side toward the camera, facing screen-right (the TRUE opposite side, not a mirrored copy of the left view).
POSE = the SAME STRICT T-POSE as in the approved front/back image, seen from the side: torso nearly upright (only a very slight forward lean, not hunched, not crouched), both feet flat on the floor side by side. SAME BODY PROPORTIONS AS THE APPROVED IMAGE (critical): same leg length relative to the body, same head and hat size, same size of everything worn on the back; do NOT lengthen the legs or stretch the body. The total height from the soles to the highest point is about {{FIG_PCT}} percent of the image height (the same figure size as in the approved image). Both arms are stretched fully straight out to the sides at shoulder height, so in a true side view they point straight toward the camera and straight away from it: the near arm appears foreshortened pointing at the viewer, its open hand with fingers spread and palm down close to the camera at shoulder height, and the far arm is hidden behind the body. The arms must NOT hang down, must NOT bend forward or backward, and must NOT be drawn along the body. Head level, looking forward (screen-left in the left view, screen-right in the right view).
{{HUMANOID}}
Camera: eye level, orthographic look, no perspective, no tilt, no 3/4 angle: a pure 90 degree side profile, torso and legs seen exactly from the side.
Both views at the same scale on the same floor line, whole body visible with margin, no overlap.
Fidelity: keep the concept's body build exactly (a thin lean creature stays thin and lean: NO added muscle definition, NO abs or six-pack, NO claws, NO teeth, NO extra anatomy or ornaments the concept does not show), the same face and ears, the same smooth painted surface. Only the pose changes.
Low detail: broad clean material masses, few small patterns, no fine wear, grain or noise. Keep cloth a simple plain clean shape (no ragged fringe, no loose strips). Low detail does NOT mean flat: keep the big folds and their big light and shadow shapes.
Light and shading (keep the concept's volume): hand-painted form shading with the same value contrast as the concept. Every big fold and every cloth layer reads as a solid volume with a lit plane and a large dark shadow plane: deep shadow in the fold recesses, under the cape, hood, collar, belt and sash, inside splits and openings, on the inner and lower faces of panels; lit tops on the ridges of the folds; roughly a third of each cloth surface sits in shadow, as in the concept, never one evenly lit flat colour with only thin fold lines. Leather, metal, skin and body get the same form shading. The light comes from above and slightly from the camera side and is the SAME in every view, so the left and right halves of the body are lit alike (no side light that leaves one half dark). Neutral white light: no rim light, no coloured light, no glow or bloom, no shadow cast on the background; only a faint contact shadow under the feet.
Background: perfectly flat neutral mid-grey.
{{REMOVE}} Hands empty and open. No other loose props.
Asymmetric details stay on the same body side in every view: {{ASYMMETRY}}
No text, labels, numbers, logo, watermark or scenery. No human silhouette.
