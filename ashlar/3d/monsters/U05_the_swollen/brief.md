# U05 The Swollen A — Tripo preparation (2026-10-22)

Concept: `concept.png` = `ashlar/art/map-01-concepts/monsters/U05_the_swollen/vA/concept_r2.png` (1536x1024, two rows: H, 3/4,
LEFT, RIGHT / H, FRONT, BACK; approved, group `ashlar-chot`). Canon text: `concept_r2.prompt.txt`; checklist row U05 (fen-drowned

## Size (measured on the sheet against the H silhouettes)
- H silhouettes 365 / 363 px. Side views 359-364 px, FRONT/BACK about 362 px = about 1.0H (head top). Belly about 0.69 of the figure
  height wide; FRONT arm span about 1.5x the height.
- Canon text: crown 0.95H, belly 0.90H wide (the sheet's belly is narrower than that). Game scale is set later in the lab.

## Silhouette and build
- Pear shape: small head on a thin neck, narrow hunched shoulders, a huge round drum-tight belly, long thin wiry arms with big
  long-fingered clawed hands (as drawn), short thin legs, big bare feet.
- Head: small, sunken eyes, gaping mouth; two thick eel tails come out of the mouth and lie over the chest; long dark wet hair.
- Open rag nightshirt (peat brown and faded pink) hanging from the shoulders down the sides and the back; the belly shows in front
  with a vertical seam of red cross-stitches.
- The concept's side views throw the head back and lean forward: the T-pose keeps the head level and the torso nearly upright.

## Materials and colours
Drowned blue-green corpse skin #5E6B5A (primary; the belly paler green-white), dark peat-brown rags #3B3127 (secondary), poppy-red
stitches #B3262A (accent), dark olive eels, near-black hair.

## Asymmetry
None that matters; symmetric.

## What Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Rag strips (5-9 per garment in the concept): big thick jagged rag panels hanging straight and close to the body.
- Long loose hair: 4-6 thick clumps lying on the back of the head, neck and upper back.
- Eels arcing away from the chest in the side views: two thick smooth tapered shapes lying on the chest and the top of the belly.
- Rag tatters hanging under the T-pose arms: short sleeves hugging the upper arm.
