- Poppy-red #B3262A embroidered poppies: three on the wimple front (top centre small, one on each wing), one large on the
  back of the wimple, two large poppies and a bud on the apron hem.

## Asymmetry
Symmetric. The poppies on the apron sit at the lower front centre; side views show them at the apron's front edge.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- The wimple wings are thin starched cloth: make them a stiff solid shape with a visible thickness (like folded card),
  identical in all views.
- Bell sleeves hanging from the forearms: one thick solid sleeve shape, no fluttering edge.
- Habit side panels with pointed edges: a few clean big points only.
