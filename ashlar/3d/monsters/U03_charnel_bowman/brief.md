## Height and proportions (measured on the model views)

## Silhouette
Tall lean skeleton, olive-brown cowl-cape with hood down, long back cape panel, a big diagonal board quiver on the back with nail heads above the right shoulder, narrow loincloth.

## Materials and colours
Aged bone ivory #D8CCB0 (primary), dark yew-wood and leather brown #5A4A36 (quiver, straps; secondary), olive-brown rag cloth, rust-brown nails, one poppy-red arm band #B3262A (accent).

## Asymmetric details
- Quiver diagonal on the back, top (nail heads) behind the RIGHT shoulder (FRONT shows the nails over image-left), bottom at the left lower back. Chosen from the FRONT view and the archer logic (bow in the left hand, draw with the right); the concept's BACK view draws it mirrored.
- Red band on the LEFT upper arm (FRONT image-right); the concept's BACK view also draws it mirrored.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and what to do
- Thin bones and rib gaps: thick plain bone bars, broad ribs (partial fill acceptable).
- Ragged cape panels: few big thick pointed panels close to the body.
- Nails: four or five thick bars, no thin arrows or fletching.
- Red band tie end: one short thick end.

## Hand-held items (NOT in the T-pose; separate assets later)
