# P02 Hush-Herald, variant A: Tripo preparation (Phase A, 2026-10-22)

Concept: `concept.png` (copy of `ashlar/art/map-01-concepts/monsters/P02_hush_herald/vA/concept_r2.png`, 2171x724, approved
in group `ashlar-chot`; row H, 3/4, WEAPON, LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK). Canon text: `concept_r2.prompt.txt`.
Canon: Act I Poppy Reach. A tall veiled figure swinging a censer of poppy smoke, robes stitched with closed eyes; spreads
sleep clouds, empowers nearby Soothed. Faceless (smooth cloth); no smoke, no halo.

## Size and proportions (measured on the sheet)
- H silhouette 366 px; figure (hood tip to soles) 510-520 px = about 1.41 H. The prompt's "crown 1.22H" guide line sits
  where the hood cone meets the head-and-veil part, not at the tip. Scale is normalised later; keep the concept's ratios.
- Tall narrow cone: the pointed hood takes the top ~22 % of the figure; the body under it is a tall robed adult.
- FRONT T-pose arm line at about 37-39 % of the figure height; span about 0.79 of the figure height (about 1.0 of the
  body height to the head top under the hood).

## Silhouette and parts
- Head: ONE tall straight narrow linen cone hood (a stiff pointed cone, straight sides, sharp but not needle-thin tip) whose
  lower part drapes onto the shoulders; a linen veil panel hangs from the front of the hood over the hidden face to a point on
  the upper chest. A short red vertical stitch mark on the front of the cone.
- Moss-olive #4E5A3A ragged over-robe in tiers (shoulder mantle, mid tier, lower tier) with big jagged hem points; linen
  #E8E1D0 under-robe to the floor; wide linen bell sleeves; a long linen front stole with two vertical rows of poppy-red
  #B3262A closed-eye sigils and a dark-red centre line. At the back the hood drape forms a big linen V down the back.

## Asymmetry
Symmetric once the censer is removed (it hung from the right hand).

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Jagged tattered edges on every tier: a few big clean points per tier, thick cloth, no fringe.
- Bell sleeves hanging deep under the arms: one thick solid sleeve shape.
- Cone tip: solid, slightly rounded at the very tip so it is not a needle.
- The "†"-like marks above/below the sigils in the concept read as crosses: replaced by plain short vertical strokes
  (art direction §3.8: no crosses).

## Hand-held items (built separately)
- Brass censer #B59A5B on its long chain (closed, no smoke): REMOVE from the T-pose; separate weapon asset
  `P02_hush_herald_censer` later.
