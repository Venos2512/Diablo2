HUMANOID: no
FAMILY: A
DESIGN: W07 Shroud-Spinner, variant A: a large pale grave spider that wraps corpses in silk like burial shrouds. Plain, NON-HAIRY, low-detail body made of three masses. (1) A HUGE ROUND ABDOMEN, almost a ball, slightly egg-shaped, the biggest and highest part of the creature: ash-grey chitin (#6F6A62, PRIMARY) painted as a few broad faceted plates with a row of small low blunt bumps along its top ridge, ending at its rear-bottom in a short blunt pointed cap (the spinneret end). Three or four BROAD smooth ivory shroud-silk bands (#D8CCB0, SECONDARY) are wound tightly around the abdomen like a burial wrap: two bands cross in an X over the very top of the ball and run down its sides, one band arcs around the upper part of the ball, and one band rings the lower rear of the ball just above the spinneret cap; broad flat bands with a few large clean torn edges, no strand or thread detail, no loose ends, nothing hanging. (2) A COMPACT cephalothorax in front of and below the abdomen: an armoured head plate of the same ash-grey/brown chitin with TWO large round milky-yellow eyes (#C9B458, the ONLY ACCENT) on its front face, two short thick downward-pointing fangs under the face and, beside them, two short thick pedipalps folded down in front of the face, close to it. (3) EIGHT LONG LEGS of slightly darker warm brown-grey chitin (#65574B), each built from three broad faceted bars: a thick upper segment rising up and out from the side of the cephalothorax to a high knee joint, a middle segment going down and outward to a second joint marked by a ring, and a long tapered foot segment ending in a blunt point on the ground; segment joints are simple chunky rings. The legs are thick (the upper segment about a fifth of the abdomen's width) and taper only toward the tip; never needle-thin. Smooth hand-painted low-detail surface with clear form shading: the abdomen has a lit top and a large dark lower half, the silk bands carry their own shadow where they curve under the ball. No hair, no bristles, no extra eyes, no corpse, no egg-sacs, no web strands, no glow, no gore.
ASYMMETRY: The spider is bilaterally symmetric, and the silk wrap is made symmetric too (the same band pattern on both flanks), so LEFT and RIGHT are true mirror images of each other. Keep the same placement in every view: the two eyes and the fangs centred on the front of the head; the X of the silk bands centred on the top of the abdomen and the band pattern mirror-symmetric left/right; the spinneret cap at the bottom centre of the rear of the abdomen; legs in mirror pairs (left leg N and right leg N at the same angle and height).
REMOVE: nothing (no weapon, no prop). The silk bands are part of the body. The concept sheet draws the far legs as pale, faded, semi-transparent shapes: that is only a drawing convention, NOT part of the design; never draw faded, pale or see-through legs.
PROPORTIONS: a big round banded abdomen on long legs in a wide low splay; heights in H = adult human height, read off the concept's model views. Total height (leg tips to the top of the abdomen) about 0.78 H; the abdomen is a ball about 0.58 H across (as tall as about three quarters of the figure) and is the highest point; its lowest point (the small spinneret cap) is about 0.19 H above the ground; the cephalothorax is compact, about 0.25 H long, its top at about 27 % of the figure height down from the top, its eyes at about 37 %, its fangs ending at about 60 %. In the side views the creature is about 1.12 H long from the front leg tip to the rear leg tip (the canon's "leg span 1.15 H"); in the FRONT and BACK views the leg tips span about 1.5 H from the outermost left tip to the outermost right tip (the concept draws this wide low splay, which is the variant's signature; keep it). Each leg is about 1.1 H long along its segments; the knees stand higher than the body's leg sockets but lower than the top of the abdomen. Eight legs: four on each side, in mirror pairs, evenly fanned.
VIEWS: Heights are percentages of the figure height, 0 % = the highest point of the figure (the top of the abdomen), 100 % = the ground under the leg tips, and they are the SAME in every view. Measured on the side views (the 3D truth; two side generations and the concept's own side views agree); FRONT and BACK must agree with them. ABDOMEN: the ball spans 0 % to about 72 % in every view; the spinneret cap is only a small blunt point on the rear-bottom of the ball, its tip at about 75 %, clearly above the ground (in the side views a small bump at the rear-bottom of the ball, in BACK a small point at the bottom centre of the ball between about 66 % and 75 %, hidden in FRONT). HEAD PLATE (cephalothorax): its top at about 27 %; in the side views it sits in front of and below the abdomen, the abdomen rising high behind it; in the FRONT view it sits centred in front of the lower half of the abdomen, which shows above and around it; in the BACK view the abdomen hides the head plate completely. EYES: two eyes at about 37 % (well above the middle of the figure height), both seen in FRONT, one eye on the front of the head plate in each side view, none in BACK. PEDIPALPS AND FANGS: hang under the face, the pedipalp and fang tips at about 58 to 62 %, well above the ground; seen in FRONT and in front of the head in the side views; hidden in BACK. SILK BANDS: two bands cross in an X at the very top of the abdomen (the crossing at about 0 to 5 % in FRONT and BACK; in the side views the two bands come over the top of the ball and run down the flank, apart from each other); one broad band arcs across the upper part of the ball (its top at about 20 % in the middle of the FRONT and BACK views, its ends at the left and right edges of the ball at about 45 to 50 %); one band rings the lower rear of the ball just above the spinneret cap (about 52 to 62 % in BACK; in the side views at the lower rear of the ball; hidden in FRONT behind the head). The pattern is mirror-symmetric, at least half of the ball stays bare grey chitin, and the bands are the same number and width in every view. LEGS: every leg tip touches the ground at 100 % in every view (one ground line); every knee is between about 23 % and 40 %; the legs leave the sides of the head plate at about 40 to 55 %. In the LEFT and RIGHT views exactly FOUR legs show, the near ones, fanned evenly from front to back with their tips evenly spaced along the ground (the front leg tip is the frontmost point of the figure, ahead of the face; the rear leg tip is the rearmost point, behind the abdomen); each far leg stands exactly behind its near twin and is hidden by it. In the FRONT and BACK views the four legs of each side fan out from the body like a hand of cards, each a separate solid bar, their tips spread evenly along the ground out to the outermost tip, mirror-symmetric left and right; legs nearer the camera overlap farther legs as solid opaque shapes (in FRONT the front pair is nearest, in BACK the rear pair is nearest, and in BACK the upper leg segments come out from behind the abdomen). Nothing faded or transparent in any view.
