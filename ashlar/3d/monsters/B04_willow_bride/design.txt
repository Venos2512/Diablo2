HUMANOID: yes
DESIGN: ASHLAR side boss B04 The Willow Bride, variant A: a drowned young bride, a slender adult woman of normal human proportions, upright, sad and gentle (not evil, not sexy): a soft simple face with closed, lowered eyes and a calm closed mouth, pale grey-green drowned skin painted smooth (no veins, no wounds). The head is ONE shape, the same in every view: a plain round circlet woven from willow twigs (a thick ring around the head at brow height, with a few small olive willow leaves lying flat on it, nothing sticking out), and over it an opaque pale grey-ivory veil #A9B1A6: at the front it frames the face and lies on both shoulders, ending at the upper chest; at the back it falls from the circlet down the back as one long tapering panel ending in a point between the shoulder blades and the hips. Long dark-brown hair: two locks frame the face and end on the collarbones; the rest falls behind the shoulders as one thick mass down the back that turns into the WILLOW ROOTS: five thick twisted dark-brown root strands #4A3526 (each about as thick as her forearm, smooth painted bark, no fine texture) that run down her back and sides and spread on the floor around her like a long bridal train, together with the gown forming ONE wide flared skirt-like base that hides the legs and feet completely; the strands lie against the gown and merge into it (no long thin loose tips, no big holes between strands), with only a few small olive willow leaves lying flat on them. Gown: a long drowned grey #8E8A80 bridal gown to the floor, modest closed neckline, a narrow cloth tie at the high waist under the bust, puffed sleeves from the shoulder to the elbow, bare slender pale forearms and hands; a few large darker olive water stains on the lower skirt, painted as big clean shapes. Colours: grey gown, pale grey-ivory veil, dark-brown roots and hair, small olive leaves; no red. Smooth hand-painted low-detail surface with broad planes and big soft fold shadows exactly like the concept; no glow, no water drops, no particles.
ASYMMETRY: The design is symmetric. Keep the five root strands balanced: one down the centre of the back and two on each side (back-left and back-right), so FRONT shows strands coming out from behind both sides of the gown and BACK shows all five fanning out under the veil point. LEFT = the creature's left side toward the camera, facing screen-left; RIGHT = its right side, facing screen-right.
REMOVE: nothing is held (in the concept the hands are clasped low; in the T-pose the arms go straight out and the hands are empty and open). Remove the thin twigs with leaves that stick out sideways from the roots. Keep circlet, veil, hair and roots.
PROPORTIONS: a slender adult woman of normal human proportions, about 7.5 heads tall from the soles to the top of the head (the veil adds only a little), shoulders about 1.8 heads wide, each arm shoulder-to-fingertip about 0.44 of the body height (arm span about equals the height), slender arms and small natural hands, a normal neck mostly covered by the veil; human plantigrade legs fully hidden by the floor-length gown and the root base, so crotch and knees are not visible and need not be drawn; the gown hangs straight and symmetric to the floor.
