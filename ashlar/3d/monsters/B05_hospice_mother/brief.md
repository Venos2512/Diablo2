## Silhouette and build
- A tall faceless veiled figure: a tall linen hood/veil over the whole head, rising to a soft rounded point; a long front veil
  panel down the chest; a long back panel.
- A vast linen gown flaring into a wide bell to the floor (no legs, no feet), with two big linen sleeve tiers on each side
  (shoulder to about 42 %, then to about 72 %) out of which the arms come; deep grey-violet inner robes at the shoulders, between
  the layers, down the centre of the back and at the bottom; two gold-ochre stole bands down the front.
- Eight long thin pale arms with long-fingered soft hands (curled and grasping in the concept; open and flat in the T-pose).

## Materials and colours
Linen veil and gown #E8E1D0 (primary), deep grey-violet inner robes #4B4552 (secondary), gold-ochre stole bands (about #B8892E),
pale soft skin on the arms and hands, warm amber lamp #E0A050 (accent; the lamp is removed from the T-pose).

## Asymmetric details
The brass night-lamp hangs from a cord at her front-left (removed: separate prop). Otherwise symmetric.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and what to do
- Ragged hems, sleeve edges and veil ends: a few big thick jagged points with clean edges, hanging straight.
- Wide hanging sleeve tiers: thick panels hanging close to the body; the outer half of every arm is free of cloth.
- The lamp on a thin chain (and its glow): removed, built as a separate prop and attached in the engine (gameplay: lamps go out).
- Eight arms: each one straight and horizontal, at least a hand's width from the next; nothing between them but background
  beyond the gown's edge.

## Hand-held items (NOT in the T-pose)
