HUMANOID: no
FAMILY: W
DESIGN: P03 Hush-Moth, variant A: ONE large pale moth (the swarm is built from copies of this single model), soft, plump and eerie, very low detail. WINGS: two large rounded forewings and two smaller rounded hindwings of soft linen-white (#E8E1D0, PRIMARY), dusted with grey-lilac powder (#8F8796) toward the wing roots and edged by a broad soft grey-lilac rim band along the outer edges; each wing carries ONE big round poppy-red eye-spot (#B3262A, the ACCENT) with a darker red centre: a large spot on the outer part of each forewing and a smaller spot on each hindwing, painted the same on the upper and the under side; at most two or three broad soft vein lines per wing; no scale pattern, no dust, no particles. BODY: a plump fuzzy body: a thick collar of linen-white fluff around the head and the thorax that hangs down the chest as a short pointed white bib, and behind it a grey-lilac (#8F8796, SECONDARY) abdomen of a few broad soft segment bands tapering to a blunt point; the fluff is a few big chunky tufts with clean painted edges, never single hairs. HEAD: small and round, almost sunk in the white fluff, with TWO big round dark red-brown eyes on its front (small soft highlight) and a tiny short snout between them. ANTENNAE: two broad feathery antennae, each ONE flat leaf-shaped plume of dark grey-lilac with a lighter rim and a few broad comb ridges, rising from the top of the head. LEGS: six slim jointed legs of dark grey-lilac (#5E5866), in three mirror pairs, made chunky enough to build (simple bars with knob joints and blunt tips). Smooth hand-painted low-detail surface with clear form shading: the wings have a lit upper half and soft darker grey-lilac shading toward the roots and under the overlaps, the fluff has dark recesses under the collar and the bib. No glow, no dust cloud, no gore.
ASYMMETRY: none: the moth is bilaterally symmetric, so the LEFT and RIGHT views are mirror images of each other. Keep the same placement in every view: the two eyes centred on the front of the head; both antennae alike in a V; the wings and their eye-spots mirror images (the same spot size and place on both sides, the same spots in FRONT and BACK); the legs in mirror pairs; the abdomen on the body axis.
REMOVE: nothing (no weapon, no prop, no swarm, no dust). Not the target pose: the concept's side views draw the wings raised up and back as surfaces seen from the side, and they show the far legs as thin extra legs between the near ones; both are only the concept's drawing. Here the wings are spread in the display described under POSE and every far leg is hidden behind its near twin.
PROPORTIONS: one moth, measured on the concept's FRONT and side views in H units (H = adult human height, H silhouette 506 px). Highest point = the antenna tips at about 0.33 H; the forewing tips at about 0.29 H; wingspan about 0.75 H from forewing tip to forewing tip; head and thorax fluff top at about 0.21 H, eyes at about 0.16 H; body from the face to the abdomen tip about 0.24 H long; leg tips spread about 0.31 H wide in FRONT. The concept's own brackets say "top 0.14H" (it marks the top of the body fluff, not of the wings) and "wingspan 0.25H" (the bracket itself is drawn 0.58 H long): the drawing is kept for the proportions; the in-game size (wingspan 0.25 H) is set at import. The forewing leading edge rises about 17 degrees from the shoulder to the tip; the hindwings sit below the forewings, overlapping them a little at the roots, and slope outward and down.
VIEWS: Heights are percentages of the figure height (about 0.33 H), 0 % = the highest point (the antenna tips), 100 % = the ground under the leg tips, and they are the SAME in every view. ANTENNAE: two flat plumes rising from the top of the head in a V to their tips at 0 %, the tips about 0.23 H apart in FRONT and BACK; in the side views the near antenna hides the far one exactly, one plume rising from the head in front of the wing column and leaning slightly forward. WINGS: all four wings in one upright plane just behind the thorax; in FRONT the whole under side face-on, in BACK the whole upper side face-on, the same outline and the same spots; the forewing leading edge leaves the shoulder at about 42 % and rises outward in a straight line to the forewing tip at about 12 %, the forewing tips the left and right ends of the figure (about 0.75 H apart); the hindwings below them, from the body at about 55 % out and down to their lowest edge at about 83 %, clear above the ground and the leg tips. EYE-SPOTS: on each forewing one big round red spot (about 0.065 H across) centred at about 29 % height and about 0.25 H out from the body centre line; on each hindwing one smaller red spot (about 0.04 H across) centred at about 70 % height and about 0.17 H out from the centre line; the same four spots in FRONT and in BACK; none shows in the side views. In the LEFT and RIGHT views the near wings point straight at the camera and show end-on as ONE narrow upright column over the shoulder, just behind the head, from about 12 % down to about 83 %, about as wide as the head; the far wings are hidden exactly behind it. HEAD AND FLUFF: the fluff top at about 40 %, the eyes at about 52 %, the snout at about 60 %; in FRONT both eyes show, centred, with the white fluff collar around the head and the pointed white bib hanging down the chest to about 86 %; in the side views one eye shows, the head is the frontmost part of the body, in front of the wing column; in BACK the head is hidden behind the fluff of the thorax and only the antenna plumes show above it. ABDOMEN: in the side views it runs back and slightly down from the thorax, behind the wing column, to its blunt tip at about 91 %, the rearmost point of the body, clearly above the ground; in BACK it is a short segmented grey-lilac cone centred on the body axis, pointing at the viewer and down, from about 45 % down to its tip at about 92 %; in FRONT it is hidden behind the bib. LEGS: six legs, every leg tip on the ground at 100 %; the legs leave the underside of the thorax at about 70 %; in FRONT and BACK three legs fan out on each side like the legs of a many-legged creature, their tips spread evenly along the ground, about 0.31 H from the outermost left tip to the outermost right tip, mirror-symmetric; in the side views exactly THREE legs show, the near front leg reaching forward under the head, the near middle leg, the near hind leg reaching back under the abdomen, each far leg hidden exactly behind its near twin. Every visible leg solid and opaque.
