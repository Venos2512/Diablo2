DESIGN: ASHLAR monster F01 Body-snatcher, variant A: an ordinary adult human grave robber, a lean wiry man (no muscle definition, no bulk), standing upright for the T-pose. Head = ONE concrete shape in every view: a deep pointed soot-black hood whose tip points straight up and a little back (the tip is the highest point of the figure); the hood opening frames a plain lean pale face half in shadow (eyes in shadow, nose, thin mouth and chin lit), no hair visible; the hood falls onto the shoulders. A long soot black-brown smock #1F1D22 (PRIMARY) with short wide sleeves ending just below the elbow in a few big pointed flaps; the smock hem is cut into a few big jagged pointed panels with clean painted edges (few thick shapes, no thin strips), ending at mid-thigh in front and a little lower at the sides and back. A thick braided hemp rope #6B5F4E lies around the neck and over both shoulders like a yoke, its two ends running down the front of the chest to the belt. One narrow brown leather strap runs diagonally across the front of the chest from the left shoulder to the right side of the waist. A wide dark-brown cloth sash belt #4A3526 around the waist, knotted at the front, with one short thick hanging end. One big coiled hemp rope #6B5F4E (a single thick loop) hangs from the belt at the left front hip down to mid-thigh, lying flat against the smock. Forearms and hands bare, pale skin. Baggy muddy-brown trousers #4A3C2E, the lower legs wrapped in cross bands of brown cloth, sturdy brown leather ankle boots #5A3E2A. Colours: soot black-brown smock and hood, burlap-tan rope, brown leather and cloth, pale skin; NO red anywhere on the body (the poppy-red ribbon of the concept belongs to the removed sack). Moderately stylized, hand-painted, low detail with broad planes, but with big dark fold shadows inside the hood, under the sleeves, under the sash and between the smock panels.
