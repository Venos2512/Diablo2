HUMANOID: no
FAMILY: W
DESIGN: W04 Mire-Sucker, variant B: a giant bloated bloodsucking fly on tall stilt legs, a horse-fly / mosquito body with a spiky angular silhouette. CHITIN: dark peat-brown (#4A3526, PRIMARY), painted as in the concept with warm tan-brown highlights on broad faceted plates and dark brown recesses. HEAD: small, with TWO big round bulging dark red-brown compound eyes (#643D2F) on its front, a short crown of 3 thick thorn spikes on top, and ONE long proboscis: a single chunky tapered red-brown spike (thick at the root, blunt-pointed tip, never a needle) pointing forward and down from between the eyes. THORAX: a compact hump of layered chevron plates with a crest of about 5 short, thick, blunt thorn spikes along its top. ABDOMEN: long, swollen and hanging back and down behind the thorax, formed as TWO big smooth glossy-free blood-red lobes (#B3262A, SECONDARY; painted as in the concept, about #AA3930 with lighter top planes and a darker underside) side by side with a clear cleft between them, each lobe a smooth bulb with a few broad soft facet planes and a small blunt dark tip at its lower end; no wound, no splatter. WINGS: on each side two narrow pointed wing blades, a long upper blade and a shorter lower blade below it, both made OPAQUE smoky grey-green (#A9B1A6, the ACCENT; the concept paints them translucent, here they are solid painted surfaces of that colour) with a thick dark brown leading-edge rib carrying a few short blunt thorns, the cells painted as a few broad soft lines; never see-through, never thin as paper. LEGS: six long stilt legs of the same brown chitin in three mirror pairs, each leg a chunky jointed bar: an upper segment from the thorax out and down to a knobby joint, then a long lower segment nearly straight down to a small blunt claw foot on the ground; a few small blunt thorn spurs at the joints; thick enough to build, never wire-thin. Smooth hand-painted low-detail surface with clear form shading (lit top planes, dark undersides of the thorax and the lobes, dark recesses between the plates). No hair, no glow, no blood drops, no gore.
ASYMMETRY: none: the fly is bilaterally symmetric, so the LEFT and RIGHT views are mirror images of each other. Keep the same placement in every view: the eyes and the proboscis centred on the head; the thorn crest along the body axis; the two lobes mirror images side by side; the wings mirror images; the legs in mirror pairs.
REMOVE: nothing (no weapon, no prop). Not the target pose: the concept's side views draw the wings swept back and up as surfaces seen from the side, and they show far legs between the near ones; the concept also paints the wings translucent and the far legs paler. All of these are only the concept's drawing conventions: here the wings are spread in the display described under POSE, every wing is opaque, every visible leg is solid with the same colour, and each far leg is hidden behind its near twin.
