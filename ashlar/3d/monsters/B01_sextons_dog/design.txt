DESIGN: B01 Sexton's Dog, variant A: an old, sad, grave-risen mastiff, a very large heavy dog of Saint-Bernard build (about 0.72 H tall including the shoulder fur and 1.4 H long including the tail, H = adult human height). Massive broad head with a deep wrinkled brow, loose hanging jowls drawn as one broad plane, long soft brown ears hanging flat against the sides of the head, a short pale cream blaze down the forehead and a pale cream muzzle with a dark nose, small tired dark eyes. Very wide deep chest and heavy forequarters; thick shaggy dark-brown fur (#4A3526) built from a few big chunky tufts with clean painted edges (a ruff around the neck and chest, feathering behind the front elbows, the tail), never individual hairs. A wide flat iron-grey collar (#5A5F66) with a few rivets circles the neck; from its front centre a short chain with one large round dull-brass ring (#B59A5B), the lantern-hook, hangs on the chest down to elbow height, drawn as one chunky ring hugging the chest. Two or three large smooth pale tan-grey patches of grave soil on the rump and hind flanks. Large paws with pale toes and short dark claws. A long shaggy tail hanging down and back as one thick tapered shape. Smooth painted low-detail surface; no exposed bone, no wounds, no gore, no glow. Prologue creature: no accent colour; the brass ring is the only bright touch.
ASYMMETRY: The animal is bilaterally symmetric. Keep these placements the same in every view: the pale cream blaze is centred on the forehead and the muzzle; the brass ring hangs at the centre of the chest (seen in FRONT, hanging in front of the chest in LEFT and RIGHT, hidden in BACK); the pale grave-soil patches sit on the upper rump and hind flanks on BOTH sides (one large patch on the upper rump in each side view, patches across both hips and the base of the tail in BACK); the collar rivets run evenly around the neck.
REMOVE: nothing (no weapon). The collar and the ring stay as body parts.
PROPORTIONS: a large heavy dog: head about one third of the body length and very broad, thick short neck, deep chest, strong straight forelegs, hind legs with their natural relaxed standing angle, big paws; body (nose to rump) about 1.7 times the height at the shoulders; the tail hangs down to about hock height.
