DESIGN: Knee-high brick-red fired-clay imp (about 0.5 m tall), lean scavenger, thin body, over-long arms, big hands, short legs with big flat feet, pointed nose, small yellow eyes. A grey slate shard worn like a hat with two short ivory horn nubs sticking out at the sides. A large olive-grey cloth sack on its back, filled with 4-6 big red bricks as ONE broad mass, held by a strap across the shoulder. Colours: fired clay red-brick #9C4A2C, dark red-brown cracked glaze #5A2A1E, slate grey, olive cloth, ivory horns; kiln orange #E2622B only as 2-3 large painted crack bands (no glow), one big band down the middle of the back.
ASYMMETRY: The slate hat is tilted slightly toward the creature's left; the sack strap runs diagonally across the chest from the creature's right shoulder to its left hip; the orange crack band runs down the middle of the sack/back and is visible only from BACK (as a thin line on the sack's rear edge in LEFT and RIGHT).
REMOVE: the long nail spear held in both hands (hands empty and open).
