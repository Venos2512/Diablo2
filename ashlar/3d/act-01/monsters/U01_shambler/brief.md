## Height and proportions (measured on the model views)
- 1H silhouette = 440 px. Figure 438-440 px = **1.00 H** (top of the hair).
- About 6.5 heads tall; head low between broad raised shoulders, short neck. Arm span in the FRONT view 407 px = 0.93 H. Hands at about 15-17 % from the top. Knees at about 30 % above the soles.

## Silhouette
Broad triangular draped shoulders (olive cape + pale scarf), long ragged pale tunic below the knees, thin grey limbs with knobbly joints, big hands, mud-brown wrapped shins.

## Materials and colours
Grave-linen grey #8C8577 (tunic, sleeves, scarf; primary), peat brown #4A3526 (leg wraps, mud, rope tie; secondary), dull olive-khaki shroud cape, dead skin #A7AC94, two dull brass coins on the eyes. Prologue: no accent colour.

## Asymmetric details
Practically none. The concept's LEFT and RIGHT views are drawn half-turned with the arms pointing sideways and the cape thrown forward over the near shoulder, and the cape reaches about 82 % at the back in those views but 58-62 % in BACK: `design.txt` VIEWS fixes one length (62 %).

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and what to do
- Many jagged rag strips (tunic hem, sleeves, cape): few big thick pointed panels close to the body.
- Rope tie ends: two short thick ends.
- Sleeve flaps under the horizontal arms: thick and close under the arm, not hanging to the waist.
- Coins: flat discs on the bandage (texture, little relief).
