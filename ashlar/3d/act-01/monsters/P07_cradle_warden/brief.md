## Size (measured on the sheet against the H silhouettes)
- H silhouettes 246 / 234 px (row 1 / row 2). Model views 356-358 px = about 1.5H (bonnet top). FRONT arm span 610 px = 2.5H,
  about 1.7x the figure height.
- Canon text: crown 2.2H, body width 1.5H (the sheet is about 0.68x that). Game scale is set later in the lab.

## Silhouette and build
- Squat barrel giant of stitched linen: huge round belly, very thick sausage arms of even thickness ending in rounded dark mitten caps
  (no fingers), short stumpy legs standing under the sides of the belly, dark blunt feet with rounded toes.
- Head: smooth blank oval face under a round nursery bonnet with a wide ruffled brim and two pleated lappets on the chest.
- Cage-cradle on the upper back like a turtle shell: thick bone-tan bars, packed with a dark maroon folded blanket; dark charcoal
  shoulder straps, side vest panels and a broad belt under the cage.
- Red cross-stitch seams: centre front, rings on the arms, down the legs; two square khaki patches on the belly.

## Materials and colours
Stitched linen #E8E1D0 (primary), dark charcoal straps and weathered bone cage #3F3A34 (secondary), poppy-red stitches #B3262A
(accent), khaki patches, dark maroon-brown blanket.

## Asymmetry
Symmetric body; one small square patch on the lower back on the creature's RIGHT (BACK view: lower screen-right).

## What Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- See-through cage bars: the cage is packed full with the blanket so no background shows through; bars thick.
- Bonnet frill and lappets: one thick ruffled brim and two thick pleated panels lying on the chest (no thin loose cloth).
- Smock hem: a clean jagged hem, no strips.

## Hand-held items (NOT in the T-pose)
- None. The cage-cradle is worn on the back and STAYS. No baby or sleeper in the cradle.
