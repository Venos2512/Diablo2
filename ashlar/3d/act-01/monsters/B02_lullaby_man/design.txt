DESIGN: ASHLAR mid-boss B02 The Lullaby Man, variant A: a towering, VERY TALL AND THIN sewn "nurse" made of patched linen and stuffing, exactly the build of the concept: long narrow torso, narrow level shoulders, stick-thin bare arms of pale grey-ivory stuffed linen with long thin fingers (no muscle, no claws, no nails), and a long straight robe-dress of patched linen #E8E1D0 that falls from a brown leather waist belt straight to the floor and flares into a wide bell-shaped hem that rests on the floor and hides the legs completely (only the tips of the pale toes show under the front hem). The head is ONE shape, the same in every view: a smooth narrow pale linen face-sack (no eyes, no mouth, no face features) with one vertical seam of three small poppy-red X stitches down its middle, covered by a stiff starched linen hood-wimple that sits close around the face-sack; the hood's top edge is flat with two short pointed corners sticking up and slightly outward at the left and right top of the head (like two stiff folded corners, not ears, not horns), and in the side views the same hood shows as one pointed corner above the brow with its back falling smoothly down the back of the head; two long flat linen lappets hang from the hood straight down over the chest, one on each side of the face-sack. Worn on the back (it STAYS, it is the identity of the monster): a tall narrow CAGE-CRADLE strapped high on the upper back like a tower: an upright box frame about as wide as the shoulders, made of FOUR THICK dark-brown wooden corner posts (simple round bars, clearly thick, never thin wire) joined by three broad dark-iron bands, with a gabled (pointed) top; every post ends in one short THICK cone finial and the gable has one taller thick central cone finial; between the posts a few thick vertical bars. Inside the cage, filling most of it, EXACTLY THREE round slate-blue #5F7385 cloth bundles stacked on top of each other like three big round cushions: plain stuffed cloth shapes with a few broad red #B3262A stitched seams (no faces, no limbs, nothing alive); the lowest of the three rests on the cage floor and there is nothing below it (never a fourth bundle). Two brown leather harness straps come from the cage over both shoulders and cross in an X on the chest with a small iron ring at the crossing; a brown leather waist belt goes around the body. Wide linen bell sleeves hang from the upper arms below the arm as one thick solid bell shape with three or four big blunt points at its edge (no thin fringe), leaving the thin forearm and hand bare. The robe carries large slate-blue patches on the lower robe and vertical rows of small poppy-red X stitches (accent, under 10 %), a few large torn points at the hem only, painted as clean shapes. Colours: linen #E8E1D0 about 60 %, slate-blue #5F7385 about 30 %, poppy-red #B3262A stitches as the small accent, dark brown leather and wood, dark iron. Smooth hand-painted low-detail surface with broad planes and big soft fold shadows, exactly like the concept; no glow, no smoke, no particles.
