  from the top = about 1.95 H. The prompt asked for crown 2.20 H and cradle top 2.55 H; the drawn guide lines are not at those
  heights (model moved them). Scale is normalised later; the T-pose keeps the concept's own ratios.
- Very tall and thin: long narrow torso, stick-thin bare pale arms with long thin fingers, a long straight robe-dress that
  flares to a wide bell hem on the floor and hides the legs completely (only the toe tips show at the front hem).
- T-pose arm line at about 33-35 % of the figure height (the cage above the head makes the figure tall); arm span about
  0.74 of the figure height = about 0.92 of the body height to the hood peak.

## Silhouette and parts
- Head: a smooth narrow linen face-sack with one vertical seam of small red X stitches, under a stiff linen hood-wimple with
  two pointed corners at the top; two long flat lappets hang from the hood over the chest.
- Back-worn: a tall narrow cage-cradle (dark brown wood posts, dark iron bands, gabled top with spike finials) strapped high on
  the upper back, rising far above the head; three slate-blue sleeper cocoons with red stitched seams stacked inside. STAYS.
- Brown leather harness straps over both shoulders crossing in an X on the chest; brown leather waist belt.
- Robe: patched linen #E8E1D0, large slate-blue #5F7385 patches on the lower robe, vertical rows of poppy-red #B3262A
  X stitches. Wide bell sleeves hanging under the arms.

## Asymmetry
Nearly symmetric. Lower robe: slate-blue patches on both front sides; at the back a big patch on the creature's left rear;
stitch rows as written in design.txt.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Thin cage bars and needle-thin spike finials: thicker simple bars (fewer, as thick as a finger-width of the figure, i.e.
  clearly readable), short thick cone finials; the cage stays an open frame but with chunky bars; cocoons fill most of it.
- Ragged sleeve and hem tatters: one thick solid bell sleeve with a few big blunt points; hem with a few large points.
- Hood lappets: thick flat panels lying on the chest, not fluttering.
- Spaces between cage and back: the cage touches the back and the back of the hood (no air gap).

## Hand-held items (built separately)
None. The concept has no weapon; hands are empty.
