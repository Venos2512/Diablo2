## Silhouette
Deep pointed hood (tip straight up, a little back), wide short torn sleeves, a long soot-black smock with a jagged hem of big pointed panels, wide sash belt with a big rope coil at the left hip, baggy trousers, wrapped shins, boots.

## Materials and colours
Soot black-brown cloth #1F1D22 (smock, hood; primary), burlap/hemp tan #6B5F4E (rope yoke and coil; secondary), brown leather and cloth (sash, strap, trousers, wraps, boots), pale skin on face, forearms and hands. The red accent #B3262A of the sheet is only on the sack ribbon, so the body has no red.

## Asymmetric details (creature's own sides)
- Rope coil at the LEFT front hip (FRONT image-right).
- Narrow leather strap across the chest from the LEFT shoulder to the RIGHT waist (front only).
- Hanging end of the sash knot at the RIGHT front (FRONT image-left).
- The concept's side views are not true profiles (the arm points sideways, the rope coil shows on both sides): the VIEWS table in `design.txt` decides.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and what to do
- Jagged rag hem and sleeve flaps: a few big thick pointed panels close to the body, no thin strips.
- Rope coil: one thick loop lying flat against the smock, not swinging free.
- Sash end: one short thick piece.
- Deep hood opening: keep the face recessed but the hood a closed solid shape.

## Hand-held items (NOT in the T-pose; separate assets later)
- Short spade (left hand in the 3/4 view).
- Big lumpy hessian corpse sack tied with one dull red ribbon (over the right shoulder in the 3/4 view; drawn in the WEAPON column, so it is a separate prop, not worn).
