## Silhouette and build
- Small plump goblin-like clay imp: big head, round soft belly, thin arms and thin short legs, big bare feet; huge round burlap sack on
  the back, wider than the body and taller than the head, loot sticking out of its top (candlestick = highest point).
- Head: terracotta face, big round worried eyes, two tiny lower fangs (as drawn), big pointed ears out through the hood, two short dark
  crescent horns on the hood.
- Pale linen hooded smock, short wide sleeves, jagged hem at mid-thigh; two dark leather shoulder straps with toggles hold the sack;
  ankle wraps and thin sandal straps.
- The concept side views bend deeply at the waist with bent knees: the T-pose straightens the legs and keeps only a slight lean.

## Materials and colours
Terracotta clay skin #9C4A2C (primary), burlap sack #6B5F4E (secondary), gold coins and brass candlestick #D9A441 (accent); pale
linen smock, dull red band on the sack, dark wooden chest, pewter jug, dark brown horns and straps.

## Asymmetry
Loot on the sack top: candlestick on the creature's LEFT, pewter jug on its RIGHT, wooden chest in the middle (FRONT and BACK of the
concept agree). The red band runs over the creature's RIGHT half of the sack's back. Gold coins: the concept is inconsistent (both
sides in the side and front views, one side in the back view); fixed as two big coins on EACH side of the sack, rear half.

## What Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Small gold chain near the candlestick: left out.
- Loose coins: none; the coins are flat discs lying against the sack.
- Torn smock hem: a few big thick jagged points hanging straight.
- Thin sandal straps: painted bands on the feet.
