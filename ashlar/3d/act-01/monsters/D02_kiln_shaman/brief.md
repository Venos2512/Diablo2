## Silhouette and build
- Small stocky-lean imp, big hooded head, big hands and feet, short legs, slight forward lean under a big round pot that rises above
  the head (pot rim = highest point). No muscle, no claws, no teeth.
- Head: cream hood wrapped round the head, flat soot-black face with two small orange eyes, two slender crescent terracotta horns
  through the hood; the hood becomes a thick scarf around the neck with two ends on the chest.
- Long soot-black robe with rust-terracotta lining, wide sleeves to the forearm, jagged hem; cream bandaged shins; terracotta clay
  hands and feet.
- Pot: round-bellied terracotta pot with grey diamond patch repairs, thick stepped rim, orange inside; ropes in an X over its sides
  and over both shoulders, one ring around it, a knot at the bottom back with two short rope ends.

## Materials and colours
Soot-black robe and face #2A2426 (primary), terracotta pot and clay skin #9C4A2C (secondary), kiln orange #E2622B only in the pot
mouth, the eyes and the lining (accent), cream hood/scarf/bandages, tan rope.

## Asymmetry
Symmetric body. The ladle staff is in the RIGHT hand in the 3/4 view only (removed). Pot patches irregular.

## What Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Ragged robe hem and torn sleeve flaps: 5-7 big thick jagged points hanging straight close to the legs; sleeves end at mid-forearm in
  3-4 short thick points hugging the arm (no long flaps hanging under the T-pose arms).
- Scarf ends and rope ends: thick, short, lying on the chest / on the pot.
- Pot glow, smoke, embers: none; the pot mouth is painted solid orange.
- Pot seen through nothing: it is solid; horns pierce the hood as one fixed crescent shape per side.

## Hand-held items (NOT in the T-pose; separate weapon asset later)
- Long wooden ladle used as a staff (drawn alone in the WEAPON column of the concept).
