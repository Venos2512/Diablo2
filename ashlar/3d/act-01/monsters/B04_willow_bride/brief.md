# B04 The Willow Bride, variant A: Tripo preparation (Phase A, 2026-10-22)

Concept: `concept.png` (copy of `ashlar/art/act-01/monsters/B04_willow_bride/vA/concept.png`, 2172x724, approved in
group `ashlar-chot`; row H, 3/4, LEFT, RIGHT, FRONT, BACK). Canon text: `concept.prompt.txt` next to the source sheet.
Canon: Act I Willow Mere side boss. A drowned bride in a rotted veil whose hair has grown into willow roots. Sad first,
dangerous second. Gentle face, closed or lowered eyes, fully covered gown. No gore, no noose, no hanged body.

## Size and proportions (measured on the sheet)
- H silhouette 482 px; figure (veil top to soles) 477-482 px = about 0.99 H (prompt: crown 0.975 H, root spread 1.25 H).
- Slender adult woman of normal human proportions, about 7.5 heads; the concept stands with hands clasped low: in the T-pose
  the arms go straight out (task note), puffed sleeves to the elbow, bare pale forearms.
- Lower body: long grey gown to the floor; the root strands come out of the hair at the upper back and shoulders and run down
  behind and beside her, spreading on the floor in a wide fan (FRONT about 0.9-1.1 H wide, sides about 0.5 H behind the heels).
  Per task note the root mass is treated like a long skirt: one connected flared base with the gown that hides the legs.

## Silhouette and parts
- Head: plain circlet = a woven ring of willow twigs with a few small leaves; a pale grey-ivory veil from the circlet over the
  head, short at the front (frames the face, lies on the shoulders), long at the back (down to a point at about 60 %).
- Long dark-brown hair framing the face, falling behind the shoulders and turning into five thick brown root strands.
- Gown: drowned grey #8E8A80, modest neckline, high waist tie, puffed sleeves to the elbows, a few big darker olive water
  stains on the lower skirt. Veil #A9B1A6, roots #4A3526. No red.

## Asymmetry
Symmetric design; the root strands differ slightly left/right in the concept: keep them balanced, five thick strands.

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and the fix
- Thin twigs with small willow leaves sticking out of the roots: removed; keep at most a few leaves lying flat on the thick
  root strands and on the circlet.
- Many thin root tips fanning on the floor with gaps: five THICK strands that merge into the gown at the back and sides and
  spread on the floor as a connected flared root base (no long thin isolated tips, no big holes between strands).
- Semi-transparent veil: one opaque thin-but-solid cloth shape lying on the head, shoulders and back (no see-through).
- Loose hair strands: one thick hair mass lying on the back that becomes the roots; nothing hangs from the arms.

## Hand-held items (built separately)
None (the hands are clasped in the concept, empty in the T-pose).
