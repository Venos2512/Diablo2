# W01 Mortar-Rat B — Tripo preparation (2026-10-22)

Concept: `concept.png` = `ashlar/art/act-01/monsters/W01_mortar_rat/vB/concept_r2.png` (2172x724, one row: H, 3/4, LEFT,
RIGHT, FRONT, BACK; approved, group `ashlar-chot`). Canon text: `concept_r2.prompt.txt` next to it; checklist row W01 (hairless
grey rat the size of a cat with spines of calcified mortar from gnawing the warm ossuary walls; packs; rattles and fires the
spines in volleys). Prologue monster: NO accent colour. Flow: HUMANOID T-pose with digitigrade hind legs, as W03 Hush-wolf.

## Size (measured on the sheet against the H silhouette, not the drawn guide lines)
- H silhouette 500 px. The model views are 314 px from the soles to the ear tips = 0.63 H.
- The sheet's bracket and the canon say "crown 0.22H" (cat-sized). The drawing is about 2.9x that. Kept: the drawn proportions;
  the game scale (0.22 H) is set later in the lab, the T-pose only needs the proportions.

## Silhouette and build
- Lean long-bodied rat standing upright on its hind legs, alert; long tapered snout, two very large round flat ears, small black
  eyes. Hairless smooth skin. Short thin forelimbs with small long-fingered paws and dark claws (held up at the chest in the
  concept; straightened into the T in the T-pose).
- Two clusters of big chalk-white mortar cones: one on the upper back and shoulders, one on the hips and rump, a bare smooth
  saddle between. Thick bald tail.
- Hind legs: short thighs, knee forward, shin back to a heel just above the floor, long flat rat feet (digitigrade-type zigzag).
- The concept leans the upper body forward about 25 degrees with the head pushed forward: the T-pose keeps only a slight lean.

## Materials and colours
Mortar-grey hairless skin #A39F93 (primary), dark slate/mauve brown #4F4840 (paws, snout tip, tail, skin folds; the concept paints
it a little warmer), chalk-white mortar spines #D8CCB0, dusty pink inner ears, black eyes. No accent colour (Prologue).

## Asymmetric details
None (symmetric design; the two spine clusters are paired left/right).

## Parts Tripo cannot build, and what to do
- Whiskers: removed.
- Long thin S-curled tail: one thick tapered tail in one simple curve down and back to the floor.
- Many small spines: a few big chunky cones, four per side in each cluster, the same count in every view.
- Big thin ears: one solid flat round shape each with some thickness.
