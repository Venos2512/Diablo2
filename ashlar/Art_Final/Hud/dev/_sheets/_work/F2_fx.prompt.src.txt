STYLE CORE (ASHLAR art direction, mandatory): moderately stylized hand-painted dark fantasy in the spirit of Diablo Immortal (the Diablo III / Diablo Immortal family look). NOT photoreal, NOT Diablo IV realism, NOT cartoon, chibi, anime, cel-shaded or toy-like. Proportions follow real anatomy; exaggeration goes only to the identity features (heroes: hands, feet and class props slightly oversized; monsters: their signature features pushed further). Strong readable silhouette, clear value grouping, clean colour blocking: a dominant colour, a secondary colour and a small accent, plus neutrals. Surfaces are SIMPLE: broad painted planes with soft gradients and a few crisp highlights; detail comes from big shapes (large torn edges, plates, straps, bones, cracks, patches), never from texture. No micro texture: no pores, fabric weave, leather grain, scratches, stains, speckle noise, fine grunge or photoreal PBR. No spiky, bristly or jagged rendering: no hair-thin scratchy strokes, saw-tooth, splintered or frayed edges, crackle or busy little spikes; outlines and edges are smooth, clean and softly rounded (sharp points only where the design itself names them, such as thorns or horns), and the detail density never exceeds the approved low-detail sheets.
