SHEET CONTENT: SKILL ICONS FOR THE BRINECALLER AND THE EXHUMER (reference image 1 = the approved skill icon sheet: copy its icon style, its dark socket-face backgrounds, its flat pale-gold painting and its icon size exactly; image 2 = the approved common HUD).
Thirteen SQUARE ICONS, all EXACTLY the same square size (about 220 px), in two rows: six in row 1, seven in row 2. Each icon is full-bleed: the flat pale-gold icon painted on its own dark socket-face background exactly as in image 1, with NO frame and NO label. Square corners, no rounded corners.
Row 1, Brinecaller (sea-witch of a dead salt sea; small accents of murky teal water with foam and grey-green gritty sea-salt are allowed; her salt is crooked and uneven, never white or symmetrical): (1) a spray of sea-water with foam flying from an open hand; (2) a small figure encased in crooked grey-green salt crystals; (3) a small figure sliding low along a sheet of water, leaving a wavy trail; (4) a spiral whirlpool current opening in the ground; (5) a ring of poured grey salt on the ground seen from a high angle, a small figure standing inside it; (6) a hissing blast of lye-brine with round bubbles.
Row 2, Exhumer (grave-robber hunter with a lantern, a crossbow and a spade; her lantern light is warm and soft): (7) a plain crossbow bolt in flight with a short motion streak; (8) a silver crossbow bolt with a bright pale tip; (9) a thrown iron hook on a chain, a small figure pulled along behind it; (10) a hand lowering a dark hood over a small lantern, only a thin line of light left; (11) a dark open pit in the ground with a spade stuck in the earth beside it; (12) a lantern with its shutters thrown wide and a short wide cone of warm light; (13) a lantern wick with a small flame between an up-chevron above and a down-chevron below.
AVOID: skulls, faces, crosses, haloes, blood, white symmetrical crystals, snowflakes, clear blue water, gold filigree.
