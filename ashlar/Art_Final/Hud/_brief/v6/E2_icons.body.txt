SHEET CONTENT: SKILL ICONS FOR THE PALLBEARER AND THE OSSUARIST (reference image 1 = the approved skill icon sheet: copy its icon style, its dark socket-face backgrounds, its flat pale-gold painting and its icon size exactly; image 2 = the approved common HUD).
Thirteen SQUARE ICONS, all EXACTLY the same square size (about 220 px), in two rows: seven in row 1, six in row 2. Each icon is full-bleed: the flat pale-gold icon painted on its own dark socket-face background exactly as in image 1, with NO frame and NO label. Square corners, no rounded corners.
Row 1, Pallbearer (heavy knight of a funeral Order with a coffin-lid shield; his light is plain silver and iron, nothing glows): (1) a heavy mace coming down with a short curved impact arc; (2) a tall coffin-lid shield slammed edge-first into the ground, a crack spreading from it; (3) a small figure rushing forward behind a raised coffin-lid shield, with a motion streak; (4) a small figure carrying a long shrouded bundle over one shoulder; (5) five silver nails flying outward in a fan; (6) a small funeral handbell swinging, with two curved sound lines; (7) an open hand held flat above a small kneeling figure, sheltering it.
Row 2, Ossuarist (an old midwife of the dead who borrows souls from the Wall; every soul is a still, cold WHITE point of light, never warm): (8) a hand pulling a faint grey-white soul shape with a white point of light up out of a crack in the ground; (9) a lance of wet grey mortar and bone splinters thrusting diagonally; (10) a small figure half sunk into a stone floor with ripples around it, and a second ripple further on where she will rise; (11) a simple standing figure built of stacked bone-grey stones and bones, with a white point of light in its chest; (12) a low barricade wall of stacked bones and round pale stones; (13) a white point of light sinking into a stone floor line with a soft breath curl above it.
AVOID: skulls, faces, crosses, haloes, blood, gold filigree.
