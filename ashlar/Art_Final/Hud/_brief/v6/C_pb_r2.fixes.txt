ORIENTATION (most important): element 1 must be drawn exactly as the bottom-right of image 1 and like image 3 (another class, correct side): the bier-frame stands on the LEFT and the hooded statue is on the RIGHT, behind it, its hood at the upper right. Do not mirror it: the statue must never be on the left of the frame. Light still comes from the upper left.
Element 2, the coffin fill, has exactly TEN horizontal courses of mortar bricks and the same outline as the inside of the frame of element 1: narrow at the foot, widest at the shoulders, then a short sloped top.
Element 3, the capstone lid, is only as wide as the top of the frame, a thin slab, not a big block.
