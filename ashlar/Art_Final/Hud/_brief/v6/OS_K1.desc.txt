Use case: game-ui-kit sheet. Image 1 is the approved PC HUD layer of ASHLAR ("Until the Last"), an original dark-fantasy isometric action RPG, on black: style and material reference only. Match its hand-painted matte grey dressed stone, pale mortar joints, dark iron, its hooded stone guardian statues and its level of detail; do not copy its layout. Image 2 is an older HUD kit sketch: only its OSSUARIST - SOULS AND STRAIN vessel (middle row, third from left) matters, as the starting point of row B; ignore everything else in it.

SHEET: 16:9 landscape, flat dark warm grey backdrop (#2B2825), no environment, soft top-left light, clear form shading. Title at the top: "OS OSSUARIST — SOULS AND STRAIN". Two rows, row heads "A" and "B" at the far left.
Each row = one large CONTEXT cell at the left, then five smaller STATE cells labelled under them exactly "0 FIRM", "3 FIRM", "6 WEEPING", "8 CRACKING", "BREACH".
CONTEXT cell: the right-hand hooded stone guardian statue from image 1 (same hood, same robe, same carved pedestal, facing image-left), but instead of a round stone ring it now holds THIS ROW'S VESSEL from the outside with both stone hands. The vessel shown with five souls and low Strain. A short stretch of the stone bar end runs off to the left. No text in this cell.
STATE cells: the vessel alone, same size and same angle in all five.

THE VESSEL'S JOB: it shows two things. SOULS, 0 to 8: souls the Ossuarist has borrowed from the Wall of the dead, each a small, still, cold WHITE point of light with a faint grey-white haze, sitting in its own niche (cold, never warm, never flickering). STRAIN, 0 to 100: how much the Wall is hurt by her borrowing, shown as a single clean crack in the stone beside the niches that creeps upward as Strain rises. At WEEPING, pale grey mortar weeps from the crack in two or three smooth runnels; at CRACKING the crack reaches the top and forks; at BREACH the stone face splits open along the crack, every niche goes dark and empty, and a thin cold white light shows in the open split. Its silhouette must be clearly NOT ROUND, so it never reads like the round life globe, even in grayscale.

ROW A, "RELIQUARY TOWER": an upright carved stone reliquary with a pointed top, about 1.7 times as tall as wide, with EIGHT small round-arched niches in two columns of four. The Strain crack runs up a plain flat pilaster on its left side. States: 0 FIRM = all niches dark, no crack; 3 FIRM = three lit (bottom first), a short crack at the base; 6 WEEPING = six lit, crack half way up, mortar weeping; 8 CRACKING = all eight lit, crack to the top and forked; BREACH as described.
ROW B, "NICHE CHEST": as the Ossuarist vessel in image 2, but a little taller: a low rectangular stone chest with EIGHT round niches in two rows of four, the Strain crack climbing its right end. Same five states.

Large, simple, readable shapes; at most one repair mark per vessel; no tiny engraving. Text: only the title, the row heads A and B, and the labels given.
AVOID: round or globe-like vessel shapes, skulls, bones sticking out, faces, ghosts with faces, crosses, haloes or rings behind anything, warm or golden light, flames, gold filigree, gemstones, glossy chrome, neon, bloom, spiky or scratchy cracks.
