STYLE CORE (ASHLAR art direction, mandatory): moderately stylized hand-painted dark fantasy in the spirit of Diablo Immortal (the Diablo III / Diablo Immortal family look). NOT photoreal, NOT Diablo IV realism, NOT cartoon, chibi, anime, cel-shaded or toy-like. Proportions follow real anatomy; exaggeration goes only to the identity features (heroes: hands, feet and class props slightly oversized; monsters: their signature features pushed further). Strong readable silhouette, clear value grouping, clean colour blocking: a dominant colour, a secondary colour and a small accent, plus neutrals. Surfaces are SIMPLE: broad painted planes with soft gradients and a few crisp highlights; detail comes from big shapes (large torn edges, plates, straps, bones, cracks, patches), never from texture. No micro texture: no pores, fabric weave, leather grain, scratches, stains, speckle noise, fine grunge or photoreal PBR. No spiky, bristly or jagged rendering: no hair-thin scratchy strokes, saw-tooth, splintered or frayed edges, crackle or busy little spikes; outlines and edges are smooth, clean and softly rounded (sharp points only where the design itself names them, such as thorns or horns), and the detail density never exceeds the approved low-detail sheets. Low detail never means flat: keep clear 3D form shading with a lit side, a shadow side, big soft fold shadows and darker occlusion in deep folds. No black cartoon outlines.

CONTENT: no crosses, no real saints, prayers or religious symbols; ASHLAR's church sign is the Wedge (a keystone shape), only where the brief asks for it. No gore emphasis and no blood splatter (Act III blood accents are small painted stains only); heroes show no wounds and no blood. Adults only, except canon child and teenage NPC concepts explicitly allowed on 23/10/2026: preserve their canon age, fully clothed, age-appropriate and non-sexualised; no graphic injury or distress. No nudity, no anatomical detail, no sexualised poses; no likeness of any real person. Never stage hanged bodies.
