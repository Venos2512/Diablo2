Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one original dark fantasy creature concept sheet, landscape 16:9.
Primary subject: HORNBACK RAVAGER, a substantially new creature inspired by the color/material mood of the original Diablo II Goatman family, never a canonical monster recreation. A LOW FRONT-HEAVY UNGULATE PREDATOR on FOUR powerful hoofed legs. A long horizontal animal body with immense high muscular withers, deep narrow chest, tapered lower rump and short thick neck holding a blunt wedge muzzle low to the ground. Four articulated animal legs, thick forelegs with weight-bearing cloven hooves, shorter rear legs with strong hocks. No hands, arms, humanoid chest, biped stance, clothing, harness, armor, axe or carried object. Short animal tail. ONE single broad flattened horn grows from the center forehead, sweeps backward toward the mane and ends in a large blunt BROKEN jagged tip; no paired ram horns, no antlers, no extra spikes. Dense matted coarse mane rises as a heavy forward shoulder ridge then slopes down the back, breaking the silhouette into large clumps. Deep-set natural dark eyes and restrained visible teeth at the corners of a predatory ungulate mouth. Plausible animal anatomy, heavy grounded gait, not a conventional ram, boar, lion or rhino. Beast identity comes from four cloven feet, sloped back, broad broken central horn and thick mane. No gore.
Color mood from inspected ORIGINAL Goatman sprite: prominent warm ochre/russet and earthy umber brown mane/head, pale weathered tan-gray hide visible on muzzle, shoulders and muscular forelegs, deep dark chocolate-brown short flank and hindleg fur, dirty ivory broken horn and dark near-black hooves. Retain visible amber-ochre/russet brown warmth and pale limbs against brown torso; do not wash the whole animal into monochrome grey. The cool gray metal of the original weapon is deliberately omitted because this is an unarmed animal. Natural restrained saturation, no neon or luminous eyes. Palette interpretation is based on a general original Goatman reference, not a claimed extracted Moon Clan canonical palette.
Composition: one cohesive 16:9 sheet. Large elevated game three-quarter full-body view on the LEFT occupying about half the sheet width, camera looks downward around 50 degrees and reveals top of mane and back. RIGHT half: three smaller clear full-body views stacked vertically, FRONT at top, true SIDE profile in middle, REAR at bottom. Each small view depicts the EXACT same anatomy, one central broken horn, color placement, mane and hooves. No extra views or text. No overlap, generous clean margins, all hooves and horn inside canvas. Large view in a tense low prowling stance with all four legs logically attached and visible where perspective permits. Small views neutral grounded standing poses. Flat dark neutral gray #3A3A3A background, minimal soft contact shadows only, no scene, floor props or ground islands.
Style: semi-realistic low-detail game production creature design, broad readable sculpted forms and 2-3 major value masses; thick mane modeled in large matted clumps rather than individual hair microdetail. Matte natural materials, warm source-family brown hues preserved under soft neutral-cool light from upper left. European gothic countryside restrained horror, dirty and worn but believable. No cartoon, shiny polish, photoreal fur strand noise, bloom, rim lighting, labels, numbers, writing, watermark, logo, UI or decorative frame.
