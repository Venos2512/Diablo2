Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE original dark fantasy creature concept sheet, 16:9 landscape.
Subject: BOG BELLOWER, a completely new amphibious carrion creature inspired only by the COLOR MOOD of the original Diablo II Zombie family. It is NOT a humanoid zombie, farmer, costumed person, or re-equipped Fieldhand. It has a swollen squat low torso like a heavy sack of flesh, a broad flattened head sunk directly into the shoulder mass, a vast hanging fleshy throat apron under a horizontal mouth, two massive long paddle-shaped forearms spreading wide and carrying its weight, and two tiny tucked hind legs barely visible beneath the rear belly. The body is wider than tall and crawls in heavy forward heaves; flattened paddle hands have thick fused webbing instead of human fingers. Hind limbs are short fleshy amphibian stubs, not boots or human legs. Broad throat folds form the main head silhouette. Small dull recessed eyes, slack mouth, rounded cranium, subtle carrion discoloration and intact skin. No horns, tail, armor, garments, tools or equipment. No human torso or human proportions. Grounded unsettling animal anatomy, restrained decay, no exposed viscera or gratuitous wounds. In a bellowing pose the hanging throat opens into a broad inflated sack and the paddle arms brace on the ground, without magical effects. Keep the exact same anatomy in every view.
COLOR PRIORITY: Match the original base Zombie reference bestiary/assets/references/zombie.gif (Blizzard classic animation), NOT a generic grey undead palette and NOT the earlier Fieldhand image. The original sprite has warm earthy tobacco-brown/clay flesh, dark umber creases and ochre-tan high points. Visually inspected reference with sampled non-black sprite colors: #7B5942 muted warm brown, #6B3C21 reddish umber, #6B5142 earthy midtone, #392818 dark warm shadow, #9C715A clay flesh highlight, #8C6952 light brown. These are actual source sprite pixels, not a claim of an official design palette. Translate their relationships into the creature's skin: dominant warm ochre-brown swollen hide, warmer clay/copper fleshy throat folds, dark umber dorsal folds and joints, small sickly muted yellow-tan high points. Preserve visible warm hue and moderate earthy saturation against the neutral background. Do not make it uniformly grey, blue, bright green or neon. No cloth material colors because it has no clothing. A damp fleshy surface with broad soft highlights, not glossy plastic or shiny slime.
Composition: One clean 16:9 sheet on flat dark neutral grey #3A3A3A, no landscape or water even though amphibious. One LARGE full-body elevated game three-quarter view occupying the left half, camera clearly from 50-60 degrees above showing the broad back and low crawl. Three smaller fully visible front, true side and rear views arranged neatly down the right half, consistent anatomy, relative scale and colors. The main view may depict the throat bellowing, while the small turnaround views show the relaxed hanging throat. Plenty of separation, no cropping, no overlap. No extra creatures, text, labels, numbers, swatches, logos or watermark.
Style/medium: Semi-realistic low-detail game concept rendering; broad massing, 2-3 large value groups, restrained surface information, no fine pore texture or excessive mottling. Worn grounded Act I gothic countryside horror through anatomy and material restraint. Neutral-cool soft upper-left light must preserve the warm original Zombie colors. Readable body against background, soft minimal contact shadows, no bloom, rim glow or cinematic darkness. Original creature design rather than canonical monster artwork.
