Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one 16:9 monster concept turnaround sheet for an original Act I action RPG creature.
Primary request: Thornback Fiend, an original reinterpretation of the Spike Fiend family, visibly different from canonical Diablo II artwork rather than a palette swap. Stocky boar-rat quadruped with massively loaded forequarters, wedge-shaped shoulders forming a broad triangular ridge of worn ivory quills, a long shovel-like muzzle, tiny recessed natural eyes, small ragged ears, broad digging forefeet, compact lower haunches and short blunt rat tail. Preserve animal plausibility, exactly four legs. Coarse brown-black pelt treated as broad clumped masses rather than individual hairs. Spines form two heavy swept-back shoulder fans meeting at a high central keel, NOT an evenly spiked hedgehog ball. Long low muzzle and shoulder wedge clearly distinguish it from a Quill Rat.
Design story and equipment: a feral former monastery orchard pest-control beast, now digging up graves along abandoned hedges. A single weathered muddy leather breast strap with an oversized corroded iron fastening and a broken trap ring sits low at the base of its neck; one short frayed remnant of dull olive wool is caught beneath it. No saddle, rider, weapons or armor plates; gear stays subordinate to the triangular shoulder silhouette. Keep the same strap, ring, quill layout, muzzle and limb anatomy in every view. This is original concept art, not canonical monster artwork.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat dark neutral grey #3A3A3A, no environment, no vignette, no gradients, no floor patches, only subtle contact shadows.
Composition: ONE wide 16:9 sheet, generous clean gutters. Top row, from left to right: a neutral grey standing adult human silhouette for scale at far left, then front, side, rear, elevated game three-quarter views of the same creature. All four turnaround creatures use the same scale; maximum quill height is 0.45 of the adult human height (human 1 H = 1.8 m; creature approximately 0.81 m). Bottom row: one attack anticipation pose bracing forelegs and lowering the long muzzle while the shoulder quill fans flare forward; one pure black creature silhouette in side three-quarter view; one clearly smaller elevated game-scale creature preview. Full bodies and all quill tips visible, no overlap, no panels or divider lines.
Style/medium: semi-realistic low-detail production asset sheet, broad readable planes, soft matte material shading, just 2-3 strong value masses, grounded European gothic countryside horror. Match project cart sheet composition and bonfire reference's restrained desaturated materials; do not copy subjects from those references.
Lighting/palette: soft neutral-cool upper-left light, gentle lower-right contact shadows, cold desaturated Act I palette. Brown-black pelt remains readable against grey, dirty ivory spines #8A8370 to #A49C86, muddy worn leather, rusty matte iron, sparse olive wool. No glowing eyes, bloom or rim lights.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, labels, logo, watermark, scenery, gratuitous gore, cartoon proportions, glossy surfaces, modern equipment, copied sprites or exact canonical designs. Preserve family identity through quadruped quill-launcher anatomy but redesign silhouette, proportions and role story.
