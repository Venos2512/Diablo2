Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset: ONE wide 16:9 landscape creature concept sheet for an original grounded Act I dark fantasy monster, Sinew Spitter.
Design: a lean nonhumanoid ground predator, radically different from a normal rogue woman or any humanoid archer. Long low wedge-shaped torso, small blunt split muzzle recessed beneath a sloping brow, four weight-bearing animal limbs with long splayed forelimbs and short folded muscular rear legs, narrow tapering hindquarters and a short low balancing tail. Its most identifiable silhouette is a pair of folded bow-shaped shoulder arches grown from its scapulae: broad curved ochre horn/hardened hide ribs fold above and alongside the shoulders, attached continuously to the body. They are anatomical spring structures, never carried bows, wings or detached weapons. Between the forelimbs beneath the chest is a clearly visible forward-facing tendon-like projectile launcher: a tense thick tan tendon cradle and a short blunt organic launch channel holding a single compact horn dart. This is an intact biological organ with tough hide covering, no exposed bloody muscle, torn flesh, guts or gore. The shoulder arches, tendon cradle, and projectile channel form one coherent tension mechanism. Clearly show the launcher opening in front and elevated views and its underside profile in side view. Predatory crawling locomotion, narrow low head and strong asymmetrical-looking folded curves in silhouette, no humanoid anatomy, human face, breasts, clothing, hands, carried bow, quiver, sword or shield. Exactly four limbs in all views and consistent anatomy and markings.
Color priority: translate the ORIGINAL Diablo II Corrupt Rogue Archer sprite family mood: warm tawny tan skin highlights (#AD8A73, #9C715A), deep brown-black leatherlike hide plates (#211C18, #211810), weathered ochre-brown arches (#634D39), warm brown midtones (#6B5142), muted darker brown shadow planes. Warm tan flanks and forelimbs contrast against a dark brown-black dorsal hide saddle and head cap. Shoulder arches look like old brown wood in hue but are grown horn, launcher tendons are warm tan. Keep this warm flesh / very dark hide / ochre arch hierarchy visibly colored and readable, not an all-grey, all-ivory or burgundy creature. These reference colors guide mood, not exact pixel reproduction. Restrained naturally worn materials, no saturated neon or emissive effects.
Composition: a large elevated game three-quarter view from roughly 50-60 degrees occupying the left half of the sheet, and three smaller clear front, true side, and rear views spaced vertically on the right half. All views show the SAME creature, same arch attachment, muzzle, limb count, tail and launcher. Full creature visible in each view with generous margins and clear negative space inside the arches and around the front limbs. Flat dark neutral grey #3A3A3A background, no setting, no ground terrain, no panels or borders. No extra characters, no scale figure required for this V2 sheet.
Style: semi-realistic low-detail production concept, broad sculptural forms and 2-3 major value masses, soft neutral-cool upper-left lighting, gentle contact shadows. Medieval gothic countryside corruption, restrained horror, plausible organic construction, rough matte hide and dull horn. Avoid dense microtexture, shiny surfaces, modern mechanical gun components, cartoon, neon, bloom, rim lighting or all-black unreadability. No text, labels, numbers, watermark, logos, copied sprites or canonical artwork. Original creature anatomy inspired by the family color mood and ranged role only.
