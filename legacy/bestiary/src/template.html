Concept chốt (ASHLAR) and D2RR concept art

The ASHLAR world holds the Concept chốt: the 28 monster concepts the project owner approved on 2026-10-21, all marked Approved. They replace the older Dying Gods and Act I monster concepts, which are no longer in this book. The D2RR world and the Concept art collection hold the project’s other own 2D concepts: Blood Moor outpost key art and in-game mockups, camp and nature prop sheets, procedural bone/rock/tree studies, UI and HUD sheets, and generated terrain tiles. Filter by concept set, open a record for its review notes, switch between versions or tiles under the image, and enlarge any sheet to fit or actual size. Default work statuses follow the QA notes: To do marks sheets the reviews recommend regenerating. Images are WebP copies at the source resolution (terrain tiles 1024 px); the original PNGs stay in the repository and the Source PNG link opens them when this file sits at the repository root. To add or edit concepts, update legacy/bestiary/assets/CONCEPT_SOURCES.json, run python scripts/import_concepts.py, then npm run build.

Building the library

Creatures contains 215 records across five games, Items has 157 curated Diablo II records, and NPCs has 7 named characters plus a Rogue Scout hireling archetype. Environments, 3D models, Animations and VFX are ready for future records. Empty collections do not represent completed assets.

3D & animation

Open a self-contained GLB in a record to orbit, zoom, fit the camera and play embedded clips. Use the timeline and speed controls to inspect motion. Files stay in your browser session and are released when the record closes. No model or motion is invented from a reference image. GLBs requiring external textures or decoder extensions are not supported in this offline build.

Your workspace

Favorites, work statuses and notes are saved on this browser. Existing notes from the previous bestiary are retained. Export workspace to back up your work; import merges the backup into this device. Imported notes and statuses replace matching entries. Catalog export includes record metadata and source links.

Games and opening chapters

Grim Dawn: 90 Act I records from Devil’s Crossing through the Warden, including optional areas. Titan Quest: 33 Greece / Act I records; difficulty-specific bosses are labeled. Torchlight (2009): 7 verified Orden Mines entries plus 27 game-wide monster-category references with areas unverified. Dungeon Siege (2002): 24 Chapter I index records; Phrak Ballista has a campaign-location uncertainty note. These are curated enemy art references, not exhaustive spawn or variant tables. The 181 cross-game enemy records have source links. 68 have an image reference: exact wiki screenshots, clearly labeled family images, or art from the official Titan Quest manual. The other records await a verified image. No production models or animation clips have been linked.

Scope & accuracy

The Diablo II collection preserves 21 entries from Blizzard's Act I bestiary and 13 named encounters including Andariel. These are reference records, not 34 independent models. The exact spawn list by game version and difficulty has not been audited. The NPC section follows Blizzard’s seven named Act I NPCs and separately catalogs the Rogue Scout hireling archetype. Official NPC GIFs are page references, not an audited count of production animation clips. Cow Level, NPCs and summons are not included in the creature set. The item set covers Act I quest objects and a broader early-game art inventory: 99 normal base gear references selected at Quality Level 18 or below, potions, gems, low runes, scrolls, tomes, jewelry and supplies. Quality Level is not a drop threshold: location, character level, difficulty and edition affect availability, so this is not a verified Act I loot table. Lord of Destruction-only systems are labeled in record metadata. Two quest images are illustrations rather than verified inventory icons; Wirt’s Leg, Key, Arrows and Bolts have no verified icon linked.

Family groupings, appearance descriptions and motion checklists are production notes. Base references suggest comparisons, not verified rig or sprite reuse. Diablo II images belong to Blizzard Entertainment. Cross-game enemy images are sourced from wikis or the official Titan Quest manual and labeled when they show a family rather than the exact variant. No complete animation count has been established.

Source & module guide

The source project includes README.md, docs/SCHEMA.md and docs/DEVELOPMENT.md. Add records through data/collections.js, register them in data/registry.js, and rebuild the standalone HTML. The renderer supports shared metadata across asset types.
