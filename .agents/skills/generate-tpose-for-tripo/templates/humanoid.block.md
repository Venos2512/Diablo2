# HUMANOID block (only for human-type bipeds: heroes, NPCs, humanoid monsters)

Paste the section that matches the sheet into `{{HUMANOID}}` of the template. Leave `{{HUMANOID}}` empty for non-humanoid creatures.
Fill `{{PROPORTIONS}}` from the PROPORTIONS section of `design.txt` (the concept's own numbers; never "improve" them).

## For front_back.prompt.md

HUMANOID BODY RULES (critical; this image is the reference for a skeleton, so it must read like a clean neutral mannequin pose of THIS character): {{PROPORTIONS}}
- Perfectly upright and symmetric: weight evenly on both legs, NO contrapposto, NO hip tilt, NO stepping forward, NO crossed leg, no knee bent for a step or a crouch. BOTH LEGS IDENTICAL and in the neutral standing position: human legs are straight from hip to ankle with straight knees; if the concept's legs are digitigrade, hoofed or bird-like, keep that exact leg anatomy including its hock bend (never turn them into human legs) and only make both legs identical and vertical. Feet flat on the floor and parallel, pointing straight forward, the centres of the two feet about 0.15 of the body height apart (about one shoulder width, NOT a wide stance). Hips level, spine straight, head level, chin level.
- LEVEL SYMMETRIC SHOULDERS: both shoulders at exactly the same height, forming one straight horizontal line with both arms. No shrugged, sloped or drooping shoulder. A pauldron, fur mantle, strap or cape on one shoulder sits on the shoulder and does not lift or drop it. The arms leave the body AT shoulder height, never from below it.
- ARMS: each arm goes from the shoulder in one perfectly horizontal straight line to the fingertips (not drooping towards the hands, not rising). Each whole arm (shoulder to fingertip) is about 0.44 of the body height, so the arm span about equals the body height; upper arm and forearm about the same length; elbow and wrist straight; hand in line with the forearm, open and flat, palm down, fingers straight and slightly apart. Both arms identical in length and height.
- LEGS AND SKIRTS: for human legs the crotch is about half the body height and the knee about 27 percent of the height above the soles (other leg types: as PROPORTIONS says). A long skirt, robe or coat hangs straight down and symmetric, hiding the legs; a slit or opening does not open because a leg is stepping out. Boots stand flat and side by side.
- The FRONT and BACK views have the same head size, shoulder height, hip height, knee height and floor line.

## For sides.prompt.md

HUMANOID BODY RULES (critical; same character and same proportions as the approved front/back image: {{PROPORTIONS}}):
- True side view of an upright symmetric body: spine and legs vertical, BOTH LEGS IDENTICAL and standing exactly side by side, so in profile one leg hides the other and the two feet overlap in one boot (human legs straight with straight knees; a digitigrade or hoofed concept keeps its leg anatomy and hock bend exactly, both legs the same). No stride, no step forward, no knee bent for a step, no lifted heel, no hip tilt, no contrapposto. A skirt or slit does not open.
- The shoulder line sits at the same height as in the approved front view (same head-to-shoulder distance, same shoulder-to-floor distance). The near arm is the same straight horizontal arm: it points straight at the camera from the shoulder, foreshortened, hand open and flat at shoulder height, NOT hanging, NOT bent, NOT angled forward or backward, NOT drawn along the torso. The far arm is hidden behind the body.
- Same head size, hip height, knee height and leg length as the approved front/back image. Do not lengthen the legs or the neck, do not shrink the head, do not add a forward lean.
