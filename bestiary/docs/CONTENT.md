# Content boundaries

The Diablo II collection preserves 34 previously selected Act I reference entries: 21 bestiary entries plus 13 named encounters including Andariel. The multiverse pass adds 181 curated cross-game enemy records across Grim Dawn, Titan Quest, Torchlight and Dungeon Siege. These are reference indices, not counts of distinct models or verified exhaustive spawn lists; 27 Torchlight entries are game-wide wiki species/category references. Items contains 157 Diablo II art-planning records: 3 quest/special objects, 18 potions, 99 normal base gear items, 28 gems/runes, 7 utility items and 2 jewelry bases. NPCs contains the seven named Diablo II Act I characters on Blizzard’s NPC page plus a distinct Rogue Scout hireling archetype. Other collections remain empty. See MULTIVERSE.md for game boundaries.

Creature families: Imps & shamans (5), Corrupted rogues (6), Undead & skeletons (7), Spirits & dark casters (2), Wild creatures (3), Brutes & demon beasts (5), Signature enemies (3), Hostile objects (3). Pitspawn Fouldog is included under Brutes & demon beasts.

Family assignments are production groupings, not engine taxonomy. Base references suggest comparisons and do not confirm shared rigs or sprites. Suggested motions are planning prompts, not extracted animations. This item set is an art-planning reference, not a complete Act I loot table; drops vary by area, level, difficulty and edition. The 99 normal bases were selected by Quality Level 18 or below, which is not an Act I drop guarantee. Two quest images are illustrations rather than inventory icons. Wirt’s Leg, Key, Arrows and Bolts have no verified image linked. There are currently zero linked production models and zero verified clips. See ITEMS.md for selection details.

Official illustrations and encounter screenshots are embedded without replacing them with invented character artwork. Source resolution may be low; pixelated enlargement preserves their appearance. Images belong to Blizzard Entertainment; provenance is recorded in assets/SOURCES.json, assets/ITEM_SOURCES.json and assets/NPC_SOURCES.json.
