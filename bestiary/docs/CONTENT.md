# Content boundaries

The initial collection preserves 34 previously selected Act I reference entries: 21 bestiary entries plus 13 named encounters including Andariel. This is a reference index, not a count of distinct models or a verified spawn list. The other asset collections begin empty.

Creature families: Imps & shamans (5), Corrupted rogues (6), Undead & skeletons (7), Spirits & dark casters (2), Wild creatures (3), Brutes & demon beasts (5), Signature enemies (3), Hostile objects (3). Pitspawn Fouldog is included under Brutes & demon beasts.

Family assignments are production groupings, not engine taxonomy. Base references suggest comparisons and do not confirm shared rigs or sprites. Suggested motions are planning prompts, not extracted animations. There are currently zero linked production models and zero verified clips.

Official illustrations and encounter screenshots are embedded without replacing them with invented character artwork. Source resolution may be low; pixelated enlargement preserves their appearance. Images belong to Blizzard Entertainment; provenance is recorded in assets/SOURCES.json.

Sources:

- https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/monsters/act1-superuniques.shtml

Descriptions and production notes were rewritten in English for the databook. Existing user notes retain their original language and content.
