# Act I NPC research and art inventory

Blizzard's [Act I NPC page](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/npcs/act1.shtml) names seven characters. The [hireling guide](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/basics/hirelings.shtml) separately describes the recruitable Rogue Scout. The databook therefore contains **seven named NPC records and one hireling archetype**, not eight named town characters.

| Record | Function and location | Art work to plan |
|---|---|---|
| Akara | Priestess, healer and magic vendor in Rogue Encampment; Den of Evil and Search for Cain | Robes, healer silhouette, talk and healing gestures, trade presentation |
| Charsi | Blacksmith, repair and equipment trade in Rogue Encampment; Tools of the Trade | Forge clothing, tools, smithing and repair interaction |
| Deckard Cain | Horadrim sage, rescued in Tristram; identifies items in camp | Captive and camp states, staff and robes, talk and identify gesture |
| Flavie | Rogue sentry warning travelers beyond the Den of Evil | Guard pose, bow/field gear, alert and warning gestures |
| Gheed | Merchant and gambler in Rogue Encampment | Distinct merchant outfit and stock, trade and gamble interactions |
| Kashya | Rogue captain and hireling recruiter in camp; Blood Raven quest | Commanding Rogue silhouette, captain details, hire interaction |
| Warriv | Caravan leader in camp; unlocks eastward travel after Act I | Travel clothing and caravan props, talk and departure presentation |
| Rogue Scout | Recruitable ranged hireling from Kashya | Generic Rogue kit, bow and run/attack/hit/death planning |

The GIFs in `assets/npcs/` are the official page references. Flavie and the Rogue Scout use the Rogue visual family; this does not establish a shared production rig. Suggested motions are art-planning prompts. No complete source animation inventory, 3D model, rig, or verified motion clip has been established. The recorded count of verified clips remains **0**.

Quest links and character roles were cross-checked against the [Act I quests](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/quests/act1.shtml) and [Act I maps](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/maps/act1.shtml). Source URLs for each GIF are in `assets/NPC_SOURCES.json`.
