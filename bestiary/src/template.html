DATABOOK GUIDE

A home for every asset.

Browse by collection, narrow by family or work status, and switch between visual cards and a compact table. Open any record for images, source links, production notes and 3D inspection.

Building the library

Creatures currently contains 34 reference records. Environments, NPCs, Items, 3D models, Animations and VFX are ready for future records. Empty collections do not represent completed assets.

3D & animation

Open a self-contained GLB in a record to orbit, zoom, fit the camera and play embedded clips. Use the timeline and speed controls to inspect motion. Files stay in your browser session and are released when the record closes. No model or motion is invented from a reference image. GLBs requiring external textures or decoder extensions are not supported in this offline build.

Your workspace

Favorites, work statuses and notes are saved on this browser. Existing notes from the previous bestiary are retained. Export workspace to back up your work; import merges the backup into this device. Imported notes and statuses replace matching entries. Catalog export includes record metadata and source links.

Scope & accuracy

The collection preserves 21 entries from Blizzard's Act I bestiary and 13 named encounters including Andariel. These are reference records, not 34 independent models. The exact spawn list by game version and difficulty has not been audited. Cow Level, NPCs and summons are not included in this initial creature set.

Family groupings, appearance descriptions and motion checklists are production notes. Base references suggest comparisons, not verified rig or sprite reuse. Images belong to Blizzard Entertainment. No complete animation count has been established.

Source & module guide

The source project includes README.md, docs/SCHEMA.md and docs/DEVELOPMENT.md. Add records through data/collections.js, register them in data/registry.js, and rebuild the standalone HTML. The renderer supports shared metadata across asset types.
