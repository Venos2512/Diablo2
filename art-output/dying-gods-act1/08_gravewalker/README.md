## Visual QA and limitations

Both saved PNGs were opened for visual inspection. Both show a recognizably human corpse with two arms and two legs, sparse hair, sunken face, empty hands, exposed grey-green flesh, ragged brown work clothes and small beige remnants. All four figures fit inside the frame without overlap. The dark neutral background, restrained horror, and absence of text, weapons, scenery, fresh blood and glow follow the brief. Flesh, brown cloth and beige accents are distinguishable by eye; no colour percentages were measured.

The initial image clearly fails the requested anatomical right-leg identity: the wrapped trailing leg in the main and front views is anatomical left. The corrective output largely retained this error rather than resolving it; the rear study also keeps the wrapped trailing leg on the viewer's left. The side study reads as a toe-dragging limp, but hidden anatomy prevents reliable side assignment from that view alone. Main/front foot contact is also flatter than the required slack toe scrape. Shoulder asymmetry is visible, but exact continuity across every small study is less certain.

The correction also warms some cloth toward reddish brown. Both images have more surface and anatomical detail than the requested broad low-to-moderate-detail treatment, and the main camera elevation is modest. Neither is a fully compliant production turnaround. The first image remains the preferred palette reference; the second records the unsuccessful single correction. No further generation was made under the one-correction limit.
