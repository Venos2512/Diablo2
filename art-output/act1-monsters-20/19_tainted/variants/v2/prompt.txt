Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE 16:9 original creature concept sheet, Storm Gullet, inspired by original Diablo II Tainted color mood.
Reference roles: original local Tainted sprite at E:/Diablo2/bestiary/assets/references/tainted.gif (frame 0 enlarged in source-tainted-frame.png) is for COLOR AND MATERIAL RELATIONSHIPS ONLY, never anatomy. Project cart/bonfire reference sheets are for restrained broad forms and lighting only. Do not use the previous generated Catacomb Tainted as a reference.
Primary design: a wholly new squat leathery TOADLIKE QUADRUPED demon with FOUR elbowed weight-bearing legs, an enormous asymmetrical throat sac, and a tiny recessed head. A low, broad, almost horizontal creature whose width exceeds its height. Small wedge of head tucked into a saddle of skin above the gullet, eyes small and recessed, short mouth under a rigid brow. No upright torso or humanoid shoulder/arm system. Both front and rear legs splay outward at elbows and fold inward at wrists/ankles, with broad three-toed clawed pads planted on ground. Hindquarters compact and heavy, no tail, no hopping cute frog pose. Belly low but clear of floor. Huge pendulous leathery throat sac bulges most strongly to the creature's anatomical LEFT, occupying nearly half its front width; the right side has compressed pleats. Preserve this anatomical handedness across views rather than mirror-flipping it. Thick large folds rather than fine warts. No biological armor spikes, wings or horn crown. Two small worn ivory jaw nubs echo the family material contrast without copying the original horned head.
Function: the gullet is a living bellows, storing charge for electrical breath. A tiny jagged ivory-yellow electrical filament forms INSIDE its slightly parted mouth, barely emerging past the lips in the main three-quarter view; no beam, cloud, aura or giant VFX. Other views show the closed resting mouth. One coherent original creature with believable joint anatomy, restrained horror, no gore.
COLOR PRIORITY: match the actual original Tainted sprite's hue relationships: limbs and lower body deep moss/forest GREEN with green-black shadows, head and upper back earthy olive/brown/ochre, pale worn dirty ivory on tiny jaw nubs and claws, a very small dried reddish-brown accent. Transfer the original olive-brown upper area to the inflated gullet and head, with warm ochre-olive lit folds against dark green legs. Brownish-red limited to mouth lining and a few healed crease marks. Keep perceptible green and ochre chroma rather than monochrome grey, plum, purple or generic brown. Worn matte leathery hide, not slick aquatic slime. This is a visual palette interpretation of a local sprite, not a claim of canonical sampled hex values. No equipment, clothing, apron, cuffs, grille, harness or carried tools.
Composition: landscape 16:9 on flat neutral dark grey #3A3A3A. One LARGE full-body elevated game THREE-QUARTER view occupying left half, approximately 50-60 degree downward game camera. On right half, three smaller full-body views of the SAME creature in orderly separated positions: FRONT at upper right, SIDE profile at middle right, REAR at lower right. Same sac side, feet, jaw nubs, skin color distribution and proportions in every view. All limbs visible or anatomically accounted for. No extra variants or detached parts. No text, headings, labels, numbers, logo, borders, watermarks or scenery. Generous margins; feet and gullet never cropped. Soft minimal ground contact shadows only.
Style and light: semi-realistic dark fantasy game asset concept, low detail with broad masses and 2-3 primary value groups; soft cool-neutral upper-left light, clear readable forms and green/ochre color separations. No bloom, rim light, hyper-detailed pores, photographic noise, cartoon styling or glossy plastic. Original reinterpretation inspired by Tainted family color mood, visibly different anatomy and locomotion from canonical bipedal Tainted and from the previous Catacomb Tainted.
