# Catacomb Tainted concept sheet

- Method: Codex CLI built-in image_gen, account-login runtime. One generation; no retries. Actual generated PNG copied unchanged into concept.png and inspected using view_image.
- Image: [concept.png](concept.png), 1672 x 941 pixels (approximately 16:9), opaque background.
- Exact actual tool prompt: [prompt.txt](prompt.txt). Saved wording used verbatim; no creative additions. Cart turnaround and bonfire images were viewed as composition/style references only.
- Original reinterpretation: Tainted-family-inspired catacomb flue-crawler with broad crouched bipedal anatomy, small swept horn ridges, scavenged rusty vent grille, leather straps, burial cloth and ankle cuffs. Intended as an original design rather than canonical monster artwork or a palette swap.

## Visual QA

All requested view categories appear, with full feet and equipment in frame, orderly separation, grey background, desaturated plum-brown skin, worn materials and restrained attack lightning. The hands remain off the floor and the broad low silhouette reads clearly. No visible text, watermark, scenery or gratuitous gore. The front monster is roughly human height, slightly taller at its grille.

Weaknesses: the skin has more fine texture and muscular surface detail than the requested low-detail 2-3-value treatment. The small rendering is roughly 240 pixels high rather than the requested approximately 80 pixels, so it is not a true target-scale readability test. The game camera is only moderately elevated. Grille geometry, strap routing, cuffs and cloth contours vary slightly between views; rear cloth coverage is more extensive than a front apron would suggest. Horn ridges are more lateral than swept back in the front view. The grey background has subtle tonal variation/contact shading rather than a verified uniform #3A3A3A fill. The lightning accent is restrained but longer than a tiny mouth-adjacent spit. Suitable as concept reference; strict orthographic modeling consistency and in-engine readability are not established.
