## Actual visual QA and limitations

The saved sheet has four views, complete framing, a dark neutral background, warm brown limbs, visibly russet shield edges and dirty ivory dorsal markings. There is no visible text, logo, watermark, scene, neon or glow. The main shield reads as flattened rather than a domed round abdomen; front knees rise conspicuously above the body. The side study reinforces the low profile. Sparse small spines/bristles and faceted chitin are visible.

The intended anatomy is two tall front legs plus six short rear walking legs. Overlap and occlusion beneath the shield make every attachment and the exact count difficult to verify in all views; this is concept art, not a validated anatomical turnaround. The front legs are longer than the rear legs, but the requested three-to-one ratio was not measured. The head is more exposed and its eyes more prominent in the large view than the prompt's tiny recessed-head ideal. The shield looks somewhat rigid and plate-like, with flexibility suggested mostly along the russet edge.

The spinner is the clearest limitation: side and rear studies show a tapered segmented appendage, but it is not unambiguously curled forward underneath and may read as a pointed tail. Silk nozzles and a tiny silk hint are not clearly resolved. Background contact shadows are present, so the requested flat #3A3A3A was not achieved as a uniform pixel color. The approximate 16:9 dimensions were accepted without resizing or altering the generated PNG. These limitations are retained honestly; no perfect compliance is claimed.
