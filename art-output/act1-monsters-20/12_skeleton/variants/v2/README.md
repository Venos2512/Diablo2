Both the actual generated source and copied concept.png were inspected with view_image.

- Four separate, fully framed views: large elevated three-quarter at left; front, side and rear stacked at right. No text, labels, borders, logos or watermarks observed.
- Clearly nonhuman quadruped, one head and four load-bearing legs. Deep coherent rib barrel, thick forelegs, smaller articulated hindquarters, short segmented tail, dark empty sockets and broad paws are readable. No weapons, shield, helmet, humanoid standing pose or supernatural glow observed.
- Warm bone palette and dark binding/rust/cloth material categories remain recognizable across views. The rib barrel, raised scapulae and front-heavy stance are broadly consistent.
- This differs from the prior humanoid Watchman premise through quadrupedal locomotion, animal skull, horizontal heavy chest silhouette and lack of soldier equipment; no direct image comparison was made.
- Not perfect construction documentation: skull is more rounded and tall, with a more tapered muzzle, than the requested low flat blunt wedge. Fine mottling and bone texture exceed the low-detail target. The scapulae are tall; the three-quarter hindquarters appear fairly high rather than deeply crouched.
- Cloth scraps are larger than tiny accents; visible bindings, scrap shapes/locations and skull proportions vary somewhat between views. Exact rib counts, digits and hidden joint connections cannot be guaranteed consistent. Joint articulation reads plausibly at concept level, not anatomically validated.
- Backdrop has soft tonal variation and floor/contact shadow rather than a strictly uniform #3A3A3A fill. No exact background-color compliance claimed.
