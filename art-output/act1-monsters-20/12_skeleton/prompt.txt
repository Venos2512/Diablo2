Use case: stylized-concept.

Background and presentation: flat uniform dark neutral grey #3A3A3A, no scenery, no panels or borders. Upper-left soft neutral-cool studio lighting and restrained soft contact shadows. All subjects fully inside canvas with generous spacing. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, labels, watermark, or logo.
Layout: exactly two rows. Top row: small plain neutral adult human scale silhouette at far left, then four larger full-body consistent views of ONE monster: front, side profile, rear, elevated game three-quarter view (~55 degrees down). Top-row monster standing height equals the adult human height: 1 H = 1.8 m, near-human proportions, not a giant. Bottom row: one full-body sword attack pose with readable anticipation, one solid black silhouette of this same monster with its sword and shield, and one small approximately 75-pixel-tall elevated game-scale rendering. Keep every view anatomically and materially consistent, sword in anatomical right hand, shield in left; the rear view mirrors screen placement naturally. No extra monsters or equipment variants.
Subject: human skeleton foot soldier, dirt-stained ivory bones with broad readable skull, ribcage, pelvis and limbs, no excessive tiny bone ornaments. A dead monastery ossuary sentry still trying to keep burial niches sealed. Distinctive low forward guard stance, skull tucked toward its raised left shoulder, long squared shoulder line broken asymmetrically by a collapsed rusted mail mantle bunched on the left shoulder and crossing diagonally behind the ribs, exposed right ribs and arm, shortened ragged mail skirt at one hip. Broken low nasal helmet with one side torn away and a broad blunt metal nasal bar, no horns or crest. Thick old leather repair straps across the mail give a clear diagonal back shape. Chipped short broad sword with squared broken tip and simple iron crossguard in right hand, no magic. Small battered shield in left hand made from a salvaged narrow ossuary niche hatch: a compact blunt pointed-arch slab of dark weathered oak with a broad rusty iron rim and one plain horizontal iron strap, one lower corner missing; no writing, symbols or decorative bone pieces. This practical hatch shield and the one-sided collapsed mail shoulder create a recognizable silhouette beyond a generic skeleton sword-and-round-shield soldier. Feet and most shin bones exposed; broad bone masses, no microscopic finger/toe detail. Bones remain naturally human, no extra limbs, skulls or growths. No glowing eyes.
Style: match the supplied project cart turnaround and bonfire style references in subdued semi-realistic 3D concept rendering, low detail broad planes, soft restrained shading, only two or three dominant value masses. Cold desaturated European gothic countryside and corrupted monastery materials: dirty ivory bones #8A8370 to #A49C86, muddy brown leather, nearly black old oak, dull rust-brown iron, grey-green shadows. No pure white bones, shiny armor, chrome, saturated accents, fine chainmail microtexture, elaborate engraving, cartoon outlines, heroic fantasy polish, rim lights, bloom, gore, or scenic background. The skeleton must remain readable against grey, including the small game view. Art direction is inspired by Act I family identity while equipment design and guard posture are original.
