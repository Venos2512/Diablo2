## Design
Original blind burrowing wedge beast with heavy shovel-like forehands, a blunt muzzle, ribbed shoulder skin folds, small hind feet, bare rump, tiny tail nub and a neck-localized needle collar. It changes silhouette and locomotion from the family's conventional ranged spiny wildlife toward a low scooping digger. It does not reproduce canonical Diablo art or sprite anatomy. No earlier concept image was used or modified.
